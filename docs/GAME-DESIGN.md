# Wickdeep — Game Design Spec

Canonical rules and numbers. Code follows this file; a balance pass changes numbers here and in code together.

## Pillars

1. **Builds are chosen, not rolled.** Paths gate boons, rooms show their reward before you enter, commitment unlocks a keystone and a weapon evolution, and every hero picks a class route (attack, skill 1, skill 2 or element) with a fork.
2. **Every element has a job.** Fire = sustained damage, Ice = control and burst, Lightning = crowds, Poison = amplifier, Holy = Smite and shields, Shadow = Reap and execute, General = crits and Bleed. Each stratum is weak to one element and resists another.
3. **Darkness is a wager.** Every floor has two optional dark rooms: harder fights for richer loot and a stepped-up reward, shown before you enter.
4. **Fights are read, not out-healed.** Low chip damage, heavy telegraphed damage, no sustain by default: nothing heals from hits or kills, and shields are windowed.

## 1. Damage model

- Foe HP = base × `1 + 0.65(s−1) + 0.08(s−1)²` × **threat HP** × party multiplier. Foe damage = base × `World.FOE_DMG` 1.4 × `1 + 0.22(s−1) + 0.03(s−1)²` × **threat damage** (1 in strata I–III, below 1 in IV). Boss HP is absolute (below), scaled by party and pact.
- **Health unit**: hero health is a small whole number (§5: Knight 36, Necromancer 18). Every foe damage value (`dmg`, `dps`, hazard ticks, boss damage) is in the same points; foe HP and hero damage are on their own scale (`HP_K` 4 converts a hero point to foe HP where a hero's damage taken or absorbed becomes foe damage or healing). Each hit rounds to a whole number, at least 1. Flat health: Vigor +2, Heartstone +4, Grail of Ages +1 per 12 kills (max +8), Philosopher's Stone +1 per 2 relics, Hearth Vitality +2 per rank, mastery 4 +1, mastery 10 +4; max health never falls under 5.
- **Threat** per stratum (`World.THREAT`): HP ×, damage ×, wave budget ×, attack-cooldown rate `cdRate` (foe cooldowns tick faster by this fraction, negative = slower, only while the foe can act; wind-ups never change; bosses are exempt), boss pause × (time between boss attacks):

Target: a Knight's mean hit from a regular or elite foe in strata I–III is 3–5% of max health (about 2 of 45); lighter heroes take relatively more; fodder and hazard ticks land the minimum 1 point; a boss blow in strata I–III costs a Knight about 2.
- Boss HP: Bone Warden 4,350 · Sporemother 5,950 · Rimeheart 16,500 · Forgelord 26,600 · Drowned King 85,700 · Hollow King 90,300. Boss base damage 2.4 (Forgelord 3.3, Drowned King 4.425, Hollow King 3.925). A foe still alive 45 s after its own spawn (`e.age`; bosses 78 s, except in strata with a boss fury (§8), where bosses are never exposed; fodder never) is **exposed**: ×1.5 damage taken, ×1.5 speed, and it loses 4% of its max HP per second (bosses 1.2%).
- Elite HP × `2.4 + 0.4(s−1)` (`World.ELITE`), damage × 0.9, radius × 1.22. Affixes: swift (×1.4 speed, wind-ups ×0.7 but never under 0.4 s), brutal (×1.4 damage), warded (ward = 45% of max HP), volatile (explodes on death), vampiric (heals 12× the damage it deals a hero: ×3 in the old health unit, times `HP_K` 4). From stratum IV an elite rolls a second, different affix (`elite2`) with chance `World.AFFIX2` 35% / 60% / 85% (IV–VI); HP and damage multipliers apply once, each affix effect once whichever slot holds it (`ED.hasAffix`); the label names both ("Swift Brutal Skeleton Knight").
- Party multiplier (n = other heroes): foe HP `World.PARTY_HP` ×1.2 / ×2.0 / ×2.6 (two, three, four heroes), wave budget `1 + 0.6n + 0.2n(n−1)`. A downed hero bleeds out in 18 s when no ally revives them (solo: at once).
- HP-relative statuses (Chill and Venom build-up, Conflagration, Wildfire) measure against foe HP before the stratum lever (bosses: `refHp`, the design HP 800–7,800), so inflating HP does not weaken them.
- Contact damage: touching a foe outside an attack state = `0.4 × dmg`, contact cooldown 1.0 s. Attack states (`lunge`, `charge`, `leap`, `bash`, `dive`) = `1.0 × dmg`. Boss contact damage × 0.8.
- Every hit's damage is the same no matter how it was produced; statuses build from **damage dealt, never from hit count**, so attack speed and damage scale them equally.

### Damage dealt by a hero (order, `hitEnemy`)

1. **Hero multiplier** (`max(0.1, dmg + Σ additions × dmgX)`): `dmg` stat, then the additions, each a row below, times `dmgX` (the product of the owned Ascended damage factors, 1 without one): conditional rows (execute: foes below 30% HP; searing: burning; lacerate: bleeding; malice: carrying Doom; brittle: chilled or frozen; fester: envenomed; opportunist: stunned, frozen or blinded; Death Knell stacks; Brand per ailment; Guardian Light; Embolden) + the class hook (`hitMul`: Momentum, Channel, White Flame, Soul Barrage…) + class-element skill hits (`classElMul`), Golden Idol (+1% per 15 party gold, at most 20%), Banner of Unity. The `dmg` stat itself is `1 + Σ additive sources`, then **× (1 + x) for each owned Ascended boon** (§3): the Ascended bucket sits after every additive source of the stat block. The additions carry `dmgX`, so an Ascended ×`dmg` card multiplies the whole multiplier: a conditional hit is exactly that factor times the same hit without the card.
2. **Crit** × `critMult` (1.75 + crit damage; chance capped at 95%), then the class `critMul`.
3. The hit's own factor `src.mul` (Cleave, Ambush, Deadeye by distance flown, Ballista's pierce ramp, Gathering Force, Gangrene, …) and, on a boss, a skill part's `bossMul`; with the Shatter boon, a hit on a frozen foe takes its bonus and ends the freeze.
4. **Foe vulnerability** `1 + Σ`: Marked (Hunter's Mark, `markV`) · Judged or `vulnT` (+0.2, once: Judgment, Hail of Thorns, smoke do not stack) · Frozen +0.25 · Sunder +0.04 per stack (own stacks, max 10) · Venom `venom/100 × (0.25 + corrode)` (full from the hero who added Venom, ×0.5 from allies). Co-op debuffs are single foe states: the strongest value wins, they never add.
5. **Element affinity** (§2): ×1.5 weak, ÷1.5 resisted (×0.667: weak cuts a fight to ⅔ of its length, resisted stretches it ×1.5). Class-skill parts tagged `el: class` take half of it (×1.25 / ×0.833); their statuses take the full value.
6. `exposed` ×1.5.
7. **Guard**: a foe with `guard {arc, mul}` takes ×mul from attacks inside the arc (damage over time, discharges and explosions ignore it; a Corroded foe has none). Emits `guardhit`.
8. `dmgTakenMul` (default 1). Invulnerable, burrowed or `invulnT` foes take 0.
9. **Ward** (warded elites) absorbs 75% of each hit until it breaks.

Status ticks, Smites, Tolls and discharges are `raw` hits: no hero multiplier, no crit, no further infusion.

### Damage taken by a hero (order, `hurtPlayer`)

1. Untouchable while: hit-invulnerable (0.6 s after a hit, 0.3 s after a hazard), dashing, or inside a skill move's i-frames (`skillIfr`).
2. Mirror Shard (25% chance to shrug off a projectile), then dodge (cap 60%, not against hazards; 0.25 s untouchable after), then Aegis Charm (blocks one hit per 10 s).
3. `amount × 60/(60 + armor) × (1 + vuln)`.
4. **Brace** (Knight, Vanguard) × the best **Sanctuary** here; their product never goes below ×0.5 (`DR_FLOOR`). Then Last Stand ×0.4, then a Judged foe's weakening (Verdict), then Chill (`chillWeaken`).
5. Rounded, at least 1. A fodder hit is then capped at `FODDER_CAP` (3%) of max HP (at least 1).
6. The **shield** absorbs first, then health. Thorns strike the attacker; a vampiric elite heals 12× the damage dealt (3 × `HP_K`).

### Shields

- Every shield a hero receives passes one sliding 10 s window in `World.addShield`, except Battle Ward: **class sources give at most 3% of max HP per window, all sources together at most 6%** (`SHIELD_WIN_CLASS`, `SHIELD_WIN_ALL`). Holy (Smite) and Bone Armor shields count only in the all-sources window.
- Sources: Rallying Charge, Shield Throw catch, Phoenix Mantle, Veil of Night, Judgment kill shield, Sacred Lantern orbs (class window); Smites (Holy); Bone Armor; Battle Ward (3% max HP when an elite room locks).
- Shields fade by 2% of max HP per second while no room is locked and outside boss rooms (`SHIELD_DECAY`). A source fills the shield only up to its cap: class sources and Bone Armor 8% of max HP (`CLASS_SHIELD_CAP`), Smites `holyCap` (6% base), Battle Ward its own 3%; a shield above its cap is never lowered. A downed or dead hero gains none.

### Healing

- No life steal: hits, kills, damage over time and shield overflow never heal. Heal sources: hearts 22% max HP + 1.5 (+3% per Forager rank); campfire 35% (once per hero); healing draught 40% (shop); fountain 50% (also raises the downed); Vigor reward 20%; Vigor boon 2; Provisions (Hearth) 2% per rank and Elixir Flask 20% at each stratum after the first; Sanctuary 7% on cast (you and allies within 180); Bone Harvest 3% per minion harvested, at most 6%; revive 35%; Second Wind and Phoenix Feather (once per descent) and Guardian Angel (once per stratum) are one-off saves.
- Healing received × `healMul` = (1 + Mending, Heartwood and Elixir bonuses) × pact.
- Hearts drop from kills (4%, elites 40%), cleared rooms (36%, +10% per Forager rank in the party, at most 80%), bosses (two) and breakable props (1%).

### Stopping shots, stuns and moves

- Enemy projectiles are stopped only by: the Shield Throw in flight, Shield Orbit, Iron Phalanx's Charge sweep, Bone Knights, and Mirror Shard (chance). No weapon deflects.
- **Hero stuns** (`stunFoe`): bosses are never stunned; elites take half; a stun sets `stunCd = dur + 2 s` and one landing while `stunCd > 0` lasts half as long; repeating sources (waves, shocks, Overload, Shield Orbit contacts, Bone Knight strikes) skip while `stunCd > 0` and set it to at least 1 s.
- **Stagger**: a Heavy weapon's direct hit stuns a non-boss foe 0.2 s (elites 0.1 s), once per 2 s per foe, never while `stunCd > 0`; it does not set `stunCd`. Hit-stop is client render only; the sim never pauses.
- **Parry** (Sworn Blade): a Cleave that starts in the last 0.3 s of a foe's wind-up cancels it and stuns 0.6 s (elites: stun 0.3 s, no cancel; never bosses), once per 2.5 s per hero and 4 s per foe.
- **Dash** (`ED.CONST.DASH`): 0.15 s at 480 px/s (72 px), untouchable for the whole dash. A hero holds `dashCharges = min(3, 1 + dashN)` charges; a spent charge refills after `dashCd = max(1.0, 1.5 × (1 − dashcdr))` s, one charge at a time. Sources: Light Step `dashN` +1 per rank (max 2), Shadow Cloak `dashcdr` 1/3 (1.0 s). A cap raised by a pick starts a refill timer; the new charge arrives one `dashCd` later.
- Skill moves (Charge, Shadowstep, Comet, Scattershot hop) never fire dash riders; their i-frames last the `iframes` seconds of the move.

**Flight shapes** of the projectile variants (generic weapon fields, `ED.WEAPONS` header):
- **Beam** (`beam`): an instant line of `speed × life` from the hero, stopped by walls and guards; it strikes every foe on it once (pierces all), in order along the line (Deadeye distance = distance along the beam). Overheat pierce makes it 50% wider per pierce. A blast, acid or Magma Heart aftershock lands on the last foe struck, else at the beam's end. A spectral strike adds a 60% hit on the same foe.
- **Lob** (`lob.t`): flies over foes to the aim point (the cursor distance; without one, the nearest foe on the aim line; clamped to 30 … `speed × life`) as a gold telegraph for `lob.t` s; the landing blast is the shot's own weapon hit (may crit, Staggers on Heavy; Deadeye distance = flight distance). Overheat only widens the blast.
- **Return** (`ret`): flies `speed × life` out, then back to the hero; it pierces all and strikes each foe once per leg. A guard or a wall turns it back. A blast triggers on each foe struck. The Shade's Whirlwind ring throws boomerangs.
- **Pool** (`pool`): where the shot ends, a ground pool deals `mul × dmg` per s for `dur` s; a holy pool shields allies (not its owner) by the Sacred Lantern rule (1.5% max health per 1.5 s per ally, 8% cap).
- **Trail** (`trail`): every `every` of flight the shot drops a cloud (`mul × dmg` per s, `dur` s), `n − 1` at most in flight, the last on impact; clouds that touch merge into one. A Grave Bolt keeps laying them.
- Bolt Thrower: each bolt is a Ballista siege bolt. Bone Splinter: each shard can become a Grave Bolt. Dawnbringer waves read `dmg`.
- Sheet model: a beam hits a third of the pack beyond its first foe (`LINE_ALL`); a trail cloud reaches 2/3 of each extra pack foe (`TRAIL_PACK`); a return weapon hits each foe twice when the foe stands inside its range.

Base hero HP: Knight 36 · Ranger 23 · Pyromancer 22 · Shade 33 · Cleric 35 · Necromancer 18 · Bellwarden 44 (armor, the Shade's 35% base dodge and Class Power: §5).

**Bell** (weapon kind `bell`, `src/sim/class-bell.js`): each bell is a point mass on a chain anchored at the hero, moving in the hero's frame (carried by the hero's step; its speed is the speed about the hero), stepped in 4 substeps a tick with no RNG.
- **Haul** (attack held, or 1.6 attack intervals after each attack press): the chain shortens to its orbit (60% of `chain`, at most 30 px, so a foe at the hero's side is in reach) and the bell is driven round the hero (spin sense fixed when the Haul starts) at 1290 × haul × (rate gain)^0.6 px/s² (rate gain = weapon rate / attack interval), against a drag of 2.5/s; the top speed is where they meet (at most 900). A hauled bell whirls over walls. Let go, the chain lets out to `chain` in 0.25 s, the bell slows and trails the hero, and walls bounce it (keeping 50% of its speed; Wrecking Bell 100%).
- **Strike**: a bell at 140 px/s or more touching a foe strikes it, each foe once per attack interval per bell, at most 60 strikes a second per hero (a 0.1 s burst): weapon dmg × clamp(speed / 320, 0.4, 1.8) through the normal hit order (crits, infusions, status build-up from damage). The hauled chain sweeping across a foe closer in (a foe at the hero's feet: its centre) strikes it the same way, once a revolution. A strike costs the bell `loss` of its speed (bosses too); knockback = weapon knock × min(1.5, the speed factor), along the swing. Each strike counts as an attack (ring, evolution, Thunder Gauntlet, Masterstroke, Twin Fang).
- **Whirl guard**: while it hauls a bell at 140 px/s or more (or a Wrecking Spin swings it) the hero takes 45% less damage (inside the damage-reduction floor). A bell at strike speed destroys enemy shots on its path and breaks props.
- Heavy and Swift bells fork by their own factors: Heavy lockout ×1.29, damage ×1.42, bell r ×1.2, cost ×0.75, chain ×1.1, haul ×0.85, knock ×1.3 (strikes Stagger); Swift lockout ×0.69, damage ×0.76, bell r ×0.85, cost ×1.1, haul ×1.25, knock ×0.5 (hauling gives Flow).
- **Sheet uptime** (`sheet.js`, `ED.Bell.busy`): casting on cooldown, the bell does not strike for a Heave's flight (Wrecker: 80 px), Wrecking Spin and reel, and for 0.4 s after each Peal (its spin-up); the weapon card notes the share, and `tools/model.js` scales the weapon by it (`_up`).
- **Sheet calibration** (`sheet.js` `BELL_EFF`): the sheet's strikes a second are the analytic model's (a standing hero, foes on the orbit) × 0.9 on one foe and × 0.92 on a pack of 4, measured in `tools/sheet-check.js`'s staged chase (the hero holds its spot and hauls, skeletons walk in); every bell weapon, Heavy and Swift stays within 10% of it.

## 2. Elements and statuses

Seven statuses: Burn, Chill (Freeze included), Charge, Venom, Radiance, Doom (element paths) and Bleed (General, physical). Elements: `fire`, `ice`, `lightning`, `poison`, `holy`, `shadow`. General and physical hits ignore affinity.

**Affinity** `elemAff(el)`: stratum `biome.weak === el` → ×1.5, `biome.resist === el` → ÷1.5 (×0.667), else ×1. It applies to that element's damage and status build-up. Each element is weak in exactly one stratum and resisted in exactly one.

| Stratum | Weak | Resist |
|---|---|---|
| I Ossuary | holy | shadow |
| II Glowmire | shadow | holy |
| III Rimefrost | fire | ice |
| IV Cinderforge | ice | fire |
| V Drowned Sanctum | lightning | poison |
| VI Starless Abyss | poison | lightning |

**Infusion**: a path gate sets an infusion stat (`fireInfuse`, `iceInfuse`, `lightningInfuse`, `poisonInfuse`, `holyInfuse`, `shadowInfuse`). Every direct hero hit (weapon, skills, dash riders, evolution strikes; minions at half strength) applies each owned infusion from the hit's damage **before** its affinity: amount = unit × rate × (1 + `statusPow`), where the unit is the damage (Burn, Charge, Radiance, Doom) or the share of the foe's HP before the stratum lever (Chill ×340, Venom ×300). Each adder applies its own affinity once. Class skill parts add rates (`infAdd`) without a gate; a class element route's weapon or minion rate counts instead of the gate's when higher. Status ticks, Smites, Tolls, discharges and explosions flagged `noProc` never infuse.

### Burn (fire)
- `burnPool += amount × aff`. Every 0.5 s the pool pays `max(1, pool × 0.2)` (never more than the pool) as Fire damage × (1 + `burnMul`), credited to the Burn owner; it never crits. Conflagration (keystone) checks after every addition.

### Chill and Freeze (ice)
- `chill += amount × (1 + chillMul) × aff × (boss 0.35 | elite 0.7 | 1)`. Chill holds 1.5 s after the last gain, then drops 25/s.
- While chilled: move speed × `1 − min(0.45, chill/180)`; wind-ups × `1 + chill/220`; foe damage × `1 − chillWeaken`.
- At chill ≥ `100 × (1 + 0.5 × freezeRes)`: frozen for `1.6 × (1 + freezeDur)` s (elites ×0.75; bosses 0.6 s), chill → 0, `freezeRes += 1` (decays 1 per 6 s). Frozen: no movement, AI or attacks; +25% damage taken.

### Charge and Discharge (lightning)
- `charge += amount × aff`. Every 1.0 s per foe (0.5 s with Tempest Arms) a charged foe discharges: `charge × (1 + shockMul)` hits it and arcs to up to `3 + arcs` nearest other foes within `140 × (1 + arcRange)`, each taking 60% (`ARC_MUL`). Arcs add no charge (duos excepted).

### Venom (poison)
- A gauge 0–100: `venom += amount × (1 + venomBuild) × aff × (boss 0.35 | elite 0.7 | 1)`. It holds 3 s (+`venomHold`) after the last gain, then falls 8/s. An envenomed foe takes up to `+25%` (+`corrode` at full Venom) from the hero who built it, half of that from allies. At 100 the foe is **Corroded**: its guard is gone.
- Moved Venom (Spore Burst, Pandemic, spreads) skips the build bonus and affinity.

### Radiance and Smite (holy)
- `rad += amount × aff`, never decays. At `SMITE_AT × the foe's real HP` (trash 12%, elite 6%, boss 2%) the owner **Smites**: Holy damage equal to the stored Radiance × (1 + `smiteMul`, + `smiteBig` on elites and bosses). Each hero within 160 of the smitten foe gains a shield of `smiteShield` (1% base, ×2 from elites and bosses, at most 3%) of max HP, once per 3 s per hero, up to `holyCap` (6% base) — Holy shields pass the all-sources window only.

### Doom, Reap and Toll (shadow)
- `doom += amount × (1 + doomMul) × aff × (elite or boss: 0.7 + doomTough)`. A non-boss foe whose HP (plus ward) falls to its Doom is **Reaped** and dies to the Doom owner. A boss is never Reaped: each time its Doom reaches 5% of its max HP it **Tolls**, taking that Doom as damage and resetting it. Reaps (of a foe that is not fodder) and Tolls feed Death Knell and Death's Edge.

### Bleed (General)
- A crit by a hero with `bleed` (or any hit with `bleedAll`) adds `hit × rate × (1 + statusPow)` to the pool. Drains like Burn (20% per 0.5 s), × (1 + `bleedMul`). Physical, no affinity.

### Ailments and spreads
- A foe's **ailments** (0–9, read as at most 4): one each for a Burn, Bleed or Charge pool worth 1% of its HP; Chill ≥ 25 or Frozen; Venom above 25; Radiance a quarter of the way to a Smite; Doom worth 2% of its HP; Dazed (stunned or blinded); Sealed. The host sends the count. Brand, Open Veins, Contagion and Gangrene read it.
- A **spread** moves pools: a recipient takes at most 25% of its own HP per pool (`SPREAD_CAP`), Frozen is never copied, each foe receives at most once per chain.
- **Chain queue**: payoffs of duos and keystones (Smite, Reap, Toll, discharge, freeze, a death) open a chain id; the payoffs they spawn queue behind it 0.08 s per hop, each foe pays each payoff kind at most once per chain id, and a trigger hero runs at most 6 entries per tick. Fodder (§8) pays and receives chains and explosions but grants no refund, shield, stack or raise.

## 3. Paths

Seven paths: six element paths and General. A hero follows at most **2 element paths** (`MAX_ELEMENTS`) or takes the **Generalist** gate and follows General alone. `affinity(path)` = sum of ranks of the hero's boons in that path (open boons count toward none; General counts only for a Generalist).

- Gate: opens the path and sets its infusion. Keystone (`key`): offered at affinity ≥ 5 (General: 6). Evolution (`evo`): its card is prepended to every offer at affinity ≥ 10 (General: 12) with the path's keystone owned and no evolution yet. Duo: both element paths at affinity ≥ 4 put their duo on every offer until it is taken.
- An element path holds 12 to 14 ranks before its evolution (Fire, Poison, Holy 12; Ice 13; Lightning, Shadow 14; General 15). Rarity: `c` common, `r` rare, `e` epic (keystones), `l` legendary (evolutions), `d` duo.
- **Class element**: Pyromancer = fire, Cleric = holy, Necromancer = shadow. Their class-skill parts deal that element without a gate; their own gate is on their start offer. Knight, Ranger and Shade have no class element (their element route is Bleed).
- **Generalist** (start offer of Knight, Ranger and Shade only): +15% damage, +5% crit chance, crits Bleed for 30% of the hit, open boons rank 1 higher; the hero never sees element boons (relics are element-free and reach every hero). Keen Eye and Brutality are open at rank 1 to element heroes.
- The sheet and offers colour each path: Fire `#ff7a2a`, Ice `#7fe3ff`, Lightning `#ffe66b`, Poison `#9be33c`, Holy `#fff0c2`, Shadow `#a070ff`, General `#d9dfe8`.

## 4. Offers

- A hero holds a queue `p.offers` of offer specs; `p.picks === p.offers.length`. Offers open only when the room is safe. API: `W.grantOffer(p, spec)`.
- Spec `src`: `start` · `level` · `room` · `class` · `forge` · `epic`.
- Cards: 3 (+1 with Crown of Choice); `epic` 4 (+1). An evolution card and a duo are prepended to `level`, `room` and `epic` offers and take no slot. A draw that finds no card (pool exhausted) shows a Vigor card.
- **Ascended pin** (`level`, `room`, `epic` only; never `start`, `class` or `forge`): when any Ascended card is eligible (§3), one of them (uniform) is slot 1 of the drawn cards, behind the prepended evolution and duo cards; the draw fills the other n − 1 slots. `p.ascN` counts the Ascended picks of the stratum (reset when a stratum starts).
- **start** (once, at run start; Head Start begins the party at a higher level, so its level offers come at once): 4 cards. Pyromancer, Cleric, Necromancer: their class gate plus 3 random other element gates. Knight, Ranger, Shade: 3 random element gates plus the Generalist gate (last).
- **level**: every even level (2, 4, 6, …). Pool: open boons, non-gate boons of the hero's own paths (a Generalist: gated General boons), element gates while a slot is free (and no Generalist gate), keystones at affinity ≥ 5 (General 6). Weight = rarity weight (`c` 60, `r` 30 + 2.5s + 60·luck, `e` 10 + 2s + 40·luck, other 10) × (own path 2.5) × (gate 0.8); at most one gate per offer. Weapon-kind boons (`pierce`, `split`) need a projectile weapon. **Hygiene**: Opportunist needs a hero that can stun, freeze or blind a foe (`W.canDaze`: a Heavy fork, Knight, Shade, an owned `iceInfuse`, `frozenHeart`, `arcStun`, `dashBlind`, `waveStun`, `sanctStun`, `cStun`, `chargeStun`, `orbitStun`, `knightStun`, `cometStun` or `parry`, or a stun or blind skill part whose stat is set: Skyfall, Verdict, Afterimage); from stratum IV the utility commons bounty, magnet, forager, fleet and mending weigh ×0.25.
- **room** (reward `boon`): the first drawn card (after a pinned Ascended card) = one unowned element gate while a slot is free, weighted by coverage (weak this or next stratum ×2, weak where an owned gate is resisted ×2, resisted next stratum ×0.5, class element ×2); the rest from the hero's own paths, then open boons at rarity ≥ r. A Generalist draws the level pool.
- **epic**: level pool, rarity ≥ r (commons fill the offer when fewer than 4 qualify), 4 cards, keystones ×2. Sources: elite-room reward (25%), boss of stratum III and V, shop (odd strata), altar.
- **Max rank and the Purse**: a card at its max rank (`ED.maxRank`) is never offered, and `W.pickBoon` grants nothing for one (it still ends the offer). A draw that finds no card (pool exhausted, e.g. a Generalist with every card maxed) shows a Purse card (`purse`, flagged `purse: true`, in no pool): picking it adds `World.rewardGold(s)` (`round((15 + 8s) × 0.65)`, the gold a gold room reward pays) to the party gold and records no rank, so it has no sheet entry, affinity or deed count.
- **forge**: up to 3 owned boons below max (no evolutions, never route or fork cards: fork ranks come from bosses, §5); picking ranks one up. None → level offer. Sources: normal-room reward (8%), rest-site anvil (odd strata), shop (even strata).
- **class**: the route system (§5): team level 3 (route, `ctier` 1) and boss I (fork, `ctier` 2). Fixed cards, no draw padding, no prepends. A class spec of a hero that already holds a fork becomes a level offer. Fork ranks 2 and 3 are not offers: bosses II and IV grant them (§5).
- Offer message to client: `{t:'offer', boons, rerolls, picks, src, path, forge, ctier, route}`. Rerolls (Q on keyboard, Y on a pad; from Insight, mastery 8 and the Four-Leaf Clover's +1 at each stratum after the first) work on every offer except forge, route and fork picks. In a party a pick untouched for 45 s is taken for the hero: the first non-evolution card (so never the Generalist gate), a random card for a route or fork pick.
- **Pacing targets**: see §10.

### Controls and aim

- Defaults (every action has two rebindable slots, keyboard codes or mouse buttons, saved in settings): move WASD / arrows; attack left mouse / J (also interacts); skill 1 Q / right mouse (rerolls boon cards while they are open); skill 2 E; dash Space; map and sheet Tab; pause Esc. Hotkeys use `e.code`. Prompts and signs show the live binding (`{action}` tokens).
- **Gamepad** (fixed layout): left stick moves, right stick aims, X or RT attacks, B or RB skill 1, Y or LT skill 2, A or LB dashes, Back opens the sheet, Start pauses. In menus and the sheet LB/RB switch tabs, B goes back, Y rerolls an offer (cycles heroes in the sheet).
- **Aim is manual.** There is no auto-aim and no aim assist. The aim origin is the projectile spawn point (hero x, y − 4). Attacks and `dir` skills fly toward the cursor; `ground` skills (Rain, Meteor, Sanctuary, Raise Legion) land on the cursor, clamped to range and cut at the first wall (chasms do not stop them; Raise Legion lands on a standable spot); `move` skills (Charge, Shadowstep) travel toward it; `self` skills ignore it. The last angle is kept when the cursor is within 12 px of the origin or outside the window; before the first pointer event the aim follows the walking direction. On a pad the right stick aims (held 0.35 s after release), else the left stick, else the last angle; ground skills reach `range × clamp((push − 0.45) / 0.5, 0.3, 1)` of the right stick's push.
- **Bell weapons ignore the aim**: holding attack (key, mouse or pad) is the Haul; the client sends it as `haulIn` (input field `h`) and the host steps the bell from it (§1, Bell). Heave flies toward the aim; Peal is `self`.

### Heavy and Swift (attack forks)

A fork replaces the weapon with a derived version (`ED.forkWeapon`, pure), so a re-tune of the base weapon carries over. Nominal damage per second is 1.1 × the base.

### Route system

- Each hero has four routes: **Attack**, **Skill 1**, **Skill 2** and **Element** (the status route: Bleed for Knight, Ranger and Shade; Fire, Holy, Shadow for the others). A route is a tier-1 card with two tier-2 forks: 12 cards per class, 84 in all (`src/data/classboons.js`).
- **Moments** (every hero, every party member): team level 3 → pick 1 of the 4 route cards; boss of stratum I → pick 1 of the route's 2 forks (rank 1); boss of stratum II → the fork gains rank 2 (a new behaviour: the card's `r2` numbers and `rider2`); boss of stratum IV → rank 3 (a transform: the `r3` numbers and the `form`, which renames the card). Ranks 2 and 3 are granted by `W.grantForkRank` with no pick, to every hero including downed and dead ones, and announced with a reveal (message `forkRank {id, rank}` to the hero, event `forkRank` to everyone); the other boss rewards are unchanged. A hero without a fork when a rank falls due banks it (`p.forkDue`) and gets the banked ranks when the fork card is picked. The forge never ranks route or fork cards. A downed hero receives the route and fork picks when back up.
- Route and fork cards never count toward path affinity and appear only in class offers. Card totals: `mods + up × (rank − 1) + r2 (rank ≥ 2) + r3 (rank 3)`. Slot damage (`s1Dmg`, `s2Dmg`) compounds card by card. Co-op cards help allies. Card text on the offer and the sheet is the exact source of every number below.
- Fork tags: Attack forks **Heavy** / **Swift**; skill forks **Burst** (damage) / **Guard** (shields, damage reduction, stuns, slows, blinds, shot swats); element forks **Focus** (single target, bosses) / **Spread** (packs).

## 6. Floors, rooms and rewards

Per stratum: start, boss, three utility rooms (treasure on odd strata or an event shrine on even strata, shop, rest site), 4 normal combat rooms (2 of them dark, §7) and elite combat `[1,1,1,1,2,2][s−1]` → 10–11 rooms. Grid bounds ±4, tree layout; a tree with fewer than 3 dead ends besides the start is redrawn. Boss = farthest dead end, BFS distance ≥ 4. Dark rooms take the farthest other dead ends, utility rooms prefer the remaining dead ends, elite rooms prefer distance ≥ 3. Floor generation is pure per `(stratum, seed)`.

- **Room sizes** (tiles of 32 px): combat and elite rooms draw from 33×17, 35×17, 31×19, 37×17, 29×19, 39×15, 33×19 (wider than the 720×420 minimum view, so the camera scrolls); boss 31×19; start, event and rest 15×11; treasure 13×11; shop 17×13. Interior patterns scale their counts and block sizes by room area / 294.
- Wave budget = `(7 + 2s)` × threat wave × party × `danger = 1 + 0.05 × distance`. From stratum II a room's roster also holds the previous biome's foes (at weight × 0.35) with chance `World.MIX_PREV` 60%. Pacing (`World.PACE`): the first wave 0.5 s after the lock, spawn telegraph 0.7–0.95 s, the next wave when at most 45% of the current one stands (gap ≥ 0.9 s) or the current one is 6 s old; foes spawn ≥ 100 px from every hero (turrets 130). Elite rooms: same budget, 2 waves + 2 elites guaranteed (3 waves under Pact 10). Wave advance counts threat foes only (never fodder).
- **Horde** (combat and elite rooms, beside the threat waves): fodder quota `World.FODDER_Q` 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 / 100 / 120 by stratum × 0.6 in an elite room × `1 + 0.5(n − 1)` per party × 0.6 on stratum I of the tutorial × 1.5 in a dark room (`DARK_FODDER`). From the first threat wave on, a pack of 6–12 (`PACK_N`) every 2–3 s (`PACK_GAP`) as one spawn zone (r 40, the spawn telegraph) at a door or wall-edge floor point of 2–4 seeded room edges, at least 220 px (`PACK_AVOID`) from every hero; a pack that finds no spot waits and the distance relaxes 40 px per 4 s of waiting (`PACK_WAIT`), down to 100 px. A pack waits while live foes + pending spawns + the pack + the threat waves still to come would pass `LIVE_CAP` (120 solo, 180 with 2+ heroes). The room clears when the last threat wave is out, the quota is spawned, no spawn zone is left and every foe is dead.
- **Room xp** (combat and elite rooms): every room drops exactly `World.ROOM_XP` (combat 24 / 38 / 60 / 84 / 107 / 122, elite 44 / 64 / 86 / 116 / 159 / 180 by stratum; × `darkLoot()` (§7) in a dark room; not scaled by the party). With every room cleared this puts the party at team level 13 on entering the stratum VI boss room (the boss kills add `def.xp × (1 + 0.3(s − 1))`): 6 `level` offers by then. `FODDER_XP_SHARE` (0.5) of it comes as motes from fodder kills (its share spread over the quota, whole units with the fraction carried). The planned wave foes share the rest: each kill drops its `World.threatXp` (`def.xp × (1 + 0.35(s − 1))`, ×3 for an elite) × `enc.xpK`, the factor that makes the planned waves fill that share exactly (fraction carried). Summons and splits in such a room drop none. A pile at the clear pays what the budget still owes. Outside these rooms (bosses, trials) a kill drops its `threatXp`.
- **Rewards** are fixed at generation and shown on doors and the map before entering:
  - normal combat: `boon` 38% · `gold` 42% · `vigor` 12% · `forge` 8%. Each kind gets its share of the floor's combat rooms (rounded by a weighted draw on the remainders). A `boon` reward is claimed by each hero as a room offer drawn from that hero's own paths; no room pays a class card.
  - elite: `relic` 75% · `epic` 25%.
- On clear, a `reward` prop appears at the room centre. Per-hero claims: `boon`, `forge`, `epic`, `vigor` (+2 max HP, heal 20%). Party claims (once): `gold` (`round((15 + 8s) × 0.65)`), `relic` (a relic hoard, one pick per hero).
- **Boss**: 2 hearts, gold and the class offer of strata I, II, IV (§5). Stratum II and IV: a relic hoard (rarity ≥ r). Stratum III and V: an `epic` offer per hero. Stratum VI ends the run.
- **Breakable props** (`biome.breakable`: I urn, II mushroom, III crystal, IV crate, V coral, VI shard): floor gen puts decor of those kinds in `room.breakables` (never baked into the floor) on plain floor off door lanes, ≥ 1.1 tiles apart, ≥ 1.5 tiles from the centre (rooms without a fight: outside the prop band); combat, elite and boss rooms hold 4–8 (topped up from their own RNG stream), other rooms at most 3. A hero hit within 9 px breaks one (melee arc, hero projectile path, team-0 explosions and skill blasts, dash path, Holy lance, Shield Charge, Shield Throw, Shadowstep; damage-over-time zones, minions and beams do not); it drops 1–3 gold 25% of the time (exact, no gold multiplier) and a heart 1% of the time, never xp. `roomState.broken` is the room's bit mask, carried in full snapshots as `BR`; `{t:'smash', i, x, y, k, r}` announces each break.

Relic, epic boon and draught prices scale 12% per stratum; every price is −4% per Merchant's Favor rank, −25% with Merchant's Ledger, ×1.4 under Pact 7, rounded to 5 (minimum 5).
- **Event shrines**: fountain (heals the party 50%, raises the downed), altar (pay 20% max HP for an epic offer), gamble shrine (`35 + 18s` gold for a random relic: 12% epic, 38% rare, 50% common), champion's trial (an elite fight for a rare relic).

## 7. Dark rooms

- Every floor flags exactly 2 normal combat rooms `dark` (`DARK_COUNT`), on dead ends other than the start and the boss, so skipping one cuts off nothing; the pick has its own stream `(seed ^ 0x2545f491) + 7907s`. They are optional: nothing needs them.
- **Read before entering**: an unvisited dark room carries a violet skull on its door preview and its map cell; the preview's line "Dark room: harder fights, richer rewards" fades in as a hero nears; reward icons show the reward after its step up.
- **Harder**: foes deal ×1.3 damage while the room is dark (`World.DARK_DMG`; minions and allies excluded); the last wave gets one more elite with an affix from brutal / vampiric / warded / swift (`World.DARK_AFFIXES`).
- **Richer**: gold and xp dropped in the room × `W.prototype.darkLoot()` = `World.DARK_LOOT` 1.5 × (1 + 0.1 × the highest Deep Wick rank in the party) (`lootMul`, the room's xp budget in `planHorde` and the Tab sheet read it); on clear the reward goes up one step, always (`World.REWARD_UP`: gold → vigor → forge → epic, boon → epic), announced by `{t:'upgrade', to, x, y}`. No extra boon offer.
- Entering an uncleared dark room emits `{t:'dark'}`. The client eases the scene darker while its hero stands in one (ambient +0.3, post-process light level ×0.7).
- There is no lantern, light, oil, flare, dread tier or Gloom.

## 8. Enemies

- Every telegraphed wind-up (lob flights included) lasts `max(t × rate, min(t, 0.4)) × e.windMul`: `rate` 0.7 for swift elites, 1 otherwise; `windMul` default 1. Foes with `e.blind > 0` cannot start attacks. Stunned or frozen foes do nothing.
- **Deeper foes aim ahead and move faster**: an aimed foe shot (shooter, orbiter burst, turret spread, caster wave and homing cast, web) leads its target by `World.LEAD` 0 / 0 / 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.7 / 0.85 (strata I–VI) of the hero's measured velocity (`p.lvx/lvy`, eased over ~0.1 s from position changes; host-side for every hero) toward the intercept point (`World.leadAngle`, flight time capped at 1.2 s); the wind-up telegraph shows the led aim. Lobbed globs and fireballs land at the target + velocity × flight × LEAD (cut back at a wall). Foe shots fly × `1 + 0.08(s − 1)` (`shotSpd`), boss shots × `1 + 0.1(s − 1)` (`bossShotSpd`), both × 1.2 under Pact 8. Non-boss, non-fodder ground speed × `1 + 0.04(s − 1)` (`foeSpd`).
- **Ranged foes shoot only on screen**: a non-boss ranged attack (shooter, turret, orbiter burst) starts only while the foe stands within the target's minimum view minus 24 px (`|dx| ≤ 336`, `|dy| ≤ 186`); a kiting foe outside it closes in.
- **Fodder** (`def.fodder`; one per biome, `biome.fodder`: Bone Mite, Sporeling, Rime Mote, Ash Imp, Brine Polyp, Void Mote): swarm brains; HP `World.fodderHp` = 7 × `1 + 0.25(s − 1)` × pact (no threat lever, party multiplier, elite or exposure); untelegraphed touch damage capped at 3% of the victim's max HP. No gold, hearts, Grail growth, splits or clouds; every kill counts for the run (`run.kills`, `p.kills`, the summary) and `run.fodderKills` counts the fodder share, while the lifetime kill stat behind the First Blood, Slayer and Legend deeds adds only the kills that are not fodder; chains and explosions run on them; each class and path owner guards its own kill hooks (no refunds, shields, stacks or minions): Death Knell and Death's Edge skip a Reaped fodder foe, the Grail skips fodder. It wears an existing foe's art (`def.art`) in its own colours at low detail (no rim light, a 1 px bar once damaged, status marks only for freeze, chill, burn and stun) and rides the snapshot as compact `H` rows `[id, type, x, y, hp/255, flags]`.
- **Crowds slow heroes**: walking speed × `max(0.5, 1 − 0.08n)` (`CONST.CROWD`), n = live non-boss foes overlapping the hero; dashes and skill moves ignore it.
- A wind-up telegraph (zone with `src`) stalls while its caster is frozen or stunned and is cancelled, without hitting, when it dies. Ground bursts placed on their own (lobbed globs, web, boss spikes, spore fields, mire, fire rain) are `detached`: they resolve on their own clock.
- **Caster wind-ups are telegraphed**: a wave cast shows a warn cone (r 230) that follows its led aim for the whole wind-up; ring and homing casts show a warn circle (caster radius + 30) on the caster.
- **No untelegraphed teleports.** When a hero is within 80 and the step is off cooldown (7 s), casters telegraph 0.6 s (marker at origin and destination) and step 150 away.
- Signature foe per biome:
  - I **Bone Priest** (support): raises a dead skeleton (telegraphed), wards an ally.
  - II **Mire Worm** (burrower): travels under ground (untargetable, visible trail), erupts under the hero after a 0.8 s ground telegraph, spore ring.
  - III **Frost Sentinel** (guard): frontal shield `guard {arc 2.2, mul 0.2}` blocks from the front, shield-bash charge.
  - IV **Ember Drone** (orbiter): circles the hero at ~160, fires 3-shot bursts.
  - V **Drowned Reaver** (leaper): leaps to the hero's position (landing circle), cleaving cone on landing.
  - VI **Rift Caller** (summoner): opens a telegraphed rift that spawns 2 starspawn every 6 s.
- Bosses use telegraphed attacks in three phases (HP 66% / 33%; the Hollow King 60% / 30%); the pause between attacks is 1.05 / 0.7 / 0.45 s by phase × 0.8 under Pact 5+ × the stratum's boss pause. After a boss falls, hostile shots and pools are removed.
  - **Aimed volley** (every boss, `World.BOSS_VOL`; an attack of weight 2 in every phase, run beside the main state): a warn line per living, visible hero follows the led aim for 0.5 s × the boss's chill wind-up factor and locks 0.15 s before the first release; then 3 / 4 / 5 shots (phase I / II / III) 0.12 s apart, each led at its release by `World.LEAD`, 0.6 × boss damage, flying `320 + 20(s − 1)` px/s (× 1.2 under Pact 8). Party: one sequence per living hero, 0.15 s apart. Freezing or stunning the boss stalls it; blinding it or a phase change cancels it.
  - **Phase 3 overlap**: in phase 3 a volley also starts every `BOSS_OV_GAP` (5 s) whatever the main attack is, never two at once.
  - **Party**: every summon list gains one entry per living hero beyond the first (at most +3), drawn from the list. Summon guards count threat foes only.
  - **Fury** (strata IV–VI; `World.BOSS_FURY` t 95 / 95 / 90 s, ramp 0.12, max ×4, warn 10 s; strata I–III never): `e.furyT` counts fight seconds from the boss's spawn, through phases, freezes, stuns and blinds. ## 11. Interface, renderer and art

### Tab sheet

### UI pixel kit

- **Grid** (`src/client/pixui.js`, `ED.PixUI`): k = floor(min(W / 480, H / 270)) device px per UI art px (2 at 1280 × 720, 4 at 1920 × 1080, 5 at 2560 × 1440), recomputed on resize and zoom; CSS `--p` = 1 art px, `--pf` = round(k / 2) device px (fine), icon sizes `--ic` 16 art px, `--ic2` 32, `--ic-f` 16 fine px. Frames, borders, sprites and offsets are whole multiples; no radius, no blur, no smooth gradient.
- **Fonts** (`src/art/fonts.css` from `art-src/fonts/` by `tools/art.js`, WOFF data URIs): every font re-gridded to 16 font px per em, so any family is crisp at font-size 16n device px. Titles, names, menu items, tabs and banners Pixel Operator Bold (700; wordmark and results word: 1 font px = 3 art px, `--fs-word` 48k; offer title `--fs-title` round(1.7 rem / 9) × 16 device px); body Pixel Operator 400/700; small Pixel Operator 8; mono Pixel Operator Mono. Text multiple t = round(1.08 rem / 16 device px): body 16 px at 720p and 1080p, 32 px at 1440p; `--fs-xl` ≈ 1.75 rem, `--fs-2xl` ≈ 2.4 rem, both whole multiples.
- **Text snap**: DOM glyph edges are whole pixels only at one origin fraction (Chromium on Windows: x + 0.5 device px; Firefox: x + 0). PixUI shifts single-line labels (menu items, buttons, titles, keycaps, `[data-px-snap]`) there with text-indent, 80 ms after DOM changes and on resize.
- **Frames** (`src/client/uiart.js`): ASCII masks with palette chars (colours may be custom properties), baked once at boot to PNG data URLs `--fr-<name>[-<variant>]`; panel (plates, toasts), btn and menu (hot, primary, danger, ghost, off states), well (fields; focus, on), kbd, caret, gem, chev, knob, ember (4-frame strip), dither. Strips animate with `steps()`. Scrims (overlays) are a flat dark wash under the 2 × 2 dither; the boon offer (93%) and the Tab sheet (96%) use the denser lock dither, so world and HUD text under them does not read. Hover = hot frame; press = 1 art px down; keyboard and pad focus = ember carets (menus) or a 1 fine-px ember outline.
- **Menu screens** (`src/client/screenart.js`, frames tab, plaque, rack, banner, glow, burst, lock, lockdither): every screen sits on a flat wash with the 2 × 2 dither; section titles, hero names, tabs and plate headers are Pixel Operator Bold at `--fs-menu` or larger, every other label is Pixel Operator 700. Tabs are open-bottomed frames: the active tab joins the dim panel below it, inactive tabs sit behind the panel's edge. Hero tiles and weapon chips: dim panel, hover brass, chosen ember panel with a gem; locked items are dithered with grey portraits and icons. **Hearth upgrades** are brick plaques with an ember set in the top course (4-frame flame, 2-frame dithered glow); a buy flashes the plaque, throws a 6-frame ember burst (0.45 s) and flashes the new rank pip; ranks are outlined pixel cells. **Armory** entries (and Armory variant chips under their base weapon) use a riveted iron rack frame instead. **Results**: the word (Victory, Fallen, Retreat, Disconnected) in Pixel Operator Bold at 1 font px = 3 art px with hard colour bands on font rows, on a cloth banner with swallow-tail ends (ember-red for a victory, ash otherwise); numbers in the number face. The title backdrop is an ordered-dither elliptical darkening baked per grid change, so it has no edge; it leaves the camp moon clear (a pixel disc on the 2 world px grid: #222222 outline, three lit bands, craters, two stepped halo rings). Under 800 px tall the Descend screen tightens (smaller portraits, tighter blocks, the solo button at the large size), so 1280 × 720 fits without scrolling. Low quality (`<html data-quality="low">`) pauses the idle animations; reduced motion stops them and drops the buy burst.

### Boon cards

- **Identity** (`src/client/cardart.js`, `ED.CardArt`): a card's kind is `ED.PickFx.kind` (fire, ice, lightning, poison, holy, shadow, general, route, duo, asc, purse); each kind has its own 9-slice frame mask (40 × 40 art px, slice 16, 8 art px of ornament outside the card box, edges tiled every 8 art px): Fire an ember-tooth crown with flame tongues and coals on soot iron; Ice crystal shards out of every corner and icicles; Lightning chamfered corners, a zigzag bolt line round every side and corner bolts; Poison a rounded frame, corner bulbs, slime and bottom drips; Holy corner pinnacles, top spires and a gothic gable crest with halo rays; Shadow torn shreds, bites out of the band, long tatters and an eye sigil crest; General riveted corner plates and rivets; route a class-coloured banner frame with spear finials, a dagged bottom, the class shield crest (class icon) with streamers and rank pips (owned, offered, empty); duo the first path's frame on the top and left and the second's on the bottom and right, split on the diagonal; Ascended a gilded frame with gold scroll brackets, prismatic wings and a taller box; purse a stitched leather pouch with metal caps and a drawstring knot. Keystones, evolutions and path gates (`PickFx.big`) add a gold rim, a gold inner line, gold corner studs, a wider card and a big socket gem.
- **Rarity** is the frame metal (common iron, rare silver-blue, epic violet, legendary gold; duo violet, Ascended gold) and the socket gem on the bottom edge (rarity colour). The path hue tints the card body (three stepped bands) and colours the icon (route: class colour, duo: the duo colour, Ascended: its rarity colour, purse gold), so path gate icons differ per path. Icons show at `--ic2` from their native 16 art px.
- **Layout**: card width a whole even number of art px (`--n` cards per row, 4 by default, 2 under 1100 px wide); five or six cards or a screen under 560 px use the dense layout (icon beside the text, 3 per row, 2 under 1100 px, 1 on a phone). The row is inset by the ornament (8 art px, 12 with Ascended wings) and cards sit two ornaments + 4 art px apart, rows 28 art px (crest over drip), so ornaments of neighbours never cross. Names are bold body text (`--fs-menu`, `--fs-xl` dense); the screen title is Pixel Operator Bold at `--fs-title`. Card text comes from the data (`ED.Sheet.text`, `ED.cardParts`, `ED.Sheet.deltas`).
- **Idle** (CSS `steps()` strips): fire sparks rise off the crown, frost creeps along the top and bottom bands, a spark runs round the lightning band, poison bubbles and drips, a slow light sweep on Holy, smoke wisps off Shadow, route streamers wave, Ascended wings shimmer through the six element colours, a glint on the purse coin; General is steady. Focus (mouse, keys, pad): the card lifts 1 art px, its frame brightens, its idle runs twice as fast, ember carets show at its sides, and `hover` plays on a change.
- **Pick**: the act is sent at once and `ED.PickFx.play` plays the kind's cue (the world pick event then stays silent); the chosen card plays its animation (0.36 s, stepped) while the others drop and fade, then the overlay hides at 0.38 s. Fire burns away bottom-up behind a burning edge; ice flashes and shatters into shards; lightning strikes with a bolt and a white flash; poison melts downward behind a melting edge; holy rises into a light pillar; shadow collapses into a turning vortex; general is stamped by a falling anvil; route unfurls the class banner; duo slams both path colours together with a flash; Ascended flashes through the prism colours; purse drops while coins cascade. An offer that arrives during the animation shows after it. Low quality and reduced motion: frames and crests stay, idle sprites hide, no effect layer, the pick is a 0.15 s flash and fade (overlay hidden at 0.16 s).
- **Fork reveal** (`#ov-fork`): the route frame in the class colour (silver at rank 2, gold at rank 3) with rank pips, unfurling downward; rank 3 names the transform in Pixel Operator Bold.

### Renderer

- The world draws into one small offscreen buffer at 1 buffer px = 1 world px (two alternating buffers); the present step upscales it to the screen (sharp-bilinear in WebGL, nearest on the 2D fallback) and applies the fractional camera offset, so scrolling stays smooth. The screen scale `R.scale` snaps to half steps so one art px (2 world px) is a whole number of screen px; the minimum view is 720 × 420 world px and the snap can show a little more.
- WebGL post (`post.js`): bloom at half the buffer, per-biome colour grade, vignette, grain, chromatic aberration. It is optional: Low quality uses plain 2D, and a software GL context switches to it at a reduced resolution.
- Cached art is a canvas first and swaps to an `ImageBitmap` once ready (`ED.Bmp`); recolours and rotated sprites are cached, never rebuilt per frame. Frame pacing: the loop measures the display rate and presents every n-th frame while frames keep missing vsync (setting Frame cap: Auto, Uncapped, 144, 120 or 60 fps).
- **Wardrobe** (`cosmetics.js`, `ED.Cos`; client only, from each hero's `p.wear`): a skin is the hero sheet recoloured by `swaps['<cls>.<a|b|secret|unkindled>']` (one variant sheet per anim, player slot and skin; portraits, the HUD medallion and lobby portraits too); a weapon sheen recolours the wielded look of that base weapon (forks and Armory variants included) by luminance onto a ramp (first weapon gold, second moonsilver, third violet; never white) and an evolution's outline sheet draws under it; Gilded is the gold ring at the feet; the Hollow Crown sits on the body frame's `anchors.head`; the Platino (`cos.platino` at full size) follows 18 px behind its hero's back with a lag (exponential, 3.5/s), hops while it moves, snaps back after a jump of 240 px, hides while its hero is down or dead and carries a faint light and eye glint for dark rooms; the coin trail stamps a flat gold footprint (3 × 5 art px, no outline or shine, alpha at most 0.5, so it never reads as loot) per 24 px walked (at most 10 a second, left and right in turn, none across a jump or while down, dead, airborne or vanished) that lives 1.6 s and fades over the last 0.6 s, at most 14 per hero (6 on Low); Last Embers replaces the death puff with rising embers; a title rides the name in the HUD hero block and party frames when it fits ("Mira the Contrarian", "Mira, One of Many"; else the bare name) and in the lobby; world nameplates show the bare name. The Blue Hearth turns the camp fire, its light and sparks blue and seats the translucent ghost delver across the fire. The Wardrobe preview (`ED.UI.wardrobePreview`) is a 16:9 canvas of the hero in the worn or hovered set beside a small hearth (its ghost under the Blue Hearth), the pet behind, footprints sliding away while it walks in place, a Last Embers cycle every 3 s and the title under it; its loop runs at 30 fps and stops while the canvas is hidden or the page is.
- **Bells** (`cls-bell.js`, `ED.ClassFx.bell`; render.js queues every bell of a living hero in the y-sorted draw list): a bell is drawn 8 px over its ground spot with a shadow and, at strike speed, a ring in the owner's colour; its chain (`fx.chain` every 16 px) runs from the hand (`Actors.hand`) to the crown, straight while taut (hauled, in a Heave, at full length), else sagging; the bell turns its crown to the hand (eased) while the chain pulls and hangs mouth down at rest; at strike speed an additive bronze smear trails it (an arc about the hero, a streak in a Heave; not on Low); a Heavy Strike flashes it white for 90 ms; the worn sheen and the evolution outline recolour it like any weapon. Outside a run (camp, Wardrobe preview, lobby) a single bell rests on the ground beside the hero on a sagging chain and small bells hang two from the fist. Events: `bstrike` (bronze sparks by speed; Heavy: flash, ring, streaks, own hit-stop and shake; `c`: the chain lashed a foe, a few iron sparks), `peal` (rings of sound in bronze and patina, the echo smaller), `bsmash`, `bhook`, `deflect` (a bronze spark near a bell); zone `bward` (patina ward ring). The Drowned Bell prop is `weapon.greatbell` at 1.5× in a drowned palette (dark patina, a crack, a glint), rocking on its lip.
- **Bell prediction** (client, `game.js`): snapshots carry each bell hero's row `P[24]` [input seq echoed, ms since the host got it, haul × 100, chain length, flags (whirl, hauled, Heave, spin, reel, spin sense), then per bell x, y from the hero's bell anchor and vx, vy]. A client steps its own bells with the same `ED.CLS.bell.tick` (and `ED.CAST.heave` / `peal`) on a stub world without foes at 60 Hz and keeps 1.5 s of history; each snapshot outside a Heave is compared with the history at the echoed input's send time plus the host's age, and the bells turn by 20% of the phase error and scale their speed 20% toward the host's; three snapshots in a row more than 1.6 rad or 60% of the chain off snap to the host. Strikes and losses the stub cannot see arrive through the corrections. Other heroes' bells interpolate between snapshots in polar form about the hero.
- **Hit feedback** (visual only): a foe flashes for 66 ms on a hit and cannot flash again until 120 ms after that flash ended; damage over time and zone ticks never flash. An ordinary foe's flash is a white silhouette over its body, a boss gets a 50% white overlay on its normal body, and the red wind-up outline shows under both. Damage numbers of one style that land within 150 ms at one spot add into a single number that pops again; a spot keeps at most 3 live numbers (the oldest goes first) and numbers over the same spot stack upward by one text height. A non-crit hit on fodder never starts a number: it only adds into a live one at its spot; a crit on fodder prints its own.

### Audio and music

- **Synthesis** (`src/client/audio.js`): every effect is oscillators and noise, no audio files. Bus gains: master slider ×0.9, music ×0.55, effects ×0.8, then a soft limiter (linear below 0.5, a tanh knee above, never past 0.86). Stingers (victory, boss, defeat) duck the music to 0.3 (defeat 0.2). Settings: master, music and effects sliders 0–100 (defaults 70 / 55 / 80).
- **Voice budget**: every oscillator or noise source is one voice; a cue of priority p spawns only while fewer than 24 / 32 / 40 / 48 voices live (p = 0 / 1 / 2 / 3). Priority 3: hero hurt, down, revive, dark door, room lock, boss roar, boss fury (a rising growl and two heartbeats), victory, defeat. 2: own crits, level, boon, relic, pick, shop. 1: own hits and kills (default). 0: other heroes' hits, foe shots, sparks, xp, gold, spawn. Each cue has a minimum gap (hit 35 ms, crit 50 ms, die 30 ms, boom 60 ms, dash-charge 80 ms, step 100 ms).
- **Per-frame aggregation**: `A.hit(crit, mine, w, el)`, `A.impact(cls, mine, el)` and `A.kill(type, r, mine)` only collect; `A.flush` (once per frame, from `ED.FX`) plays one crit cue, one own-hit cue and one cue for all other heroes' hits (half volume). Body length and pitch follow `w` (the hit's share of the foe's max health, 0.25–1.4); n hits in one frame scale loudness and body by log2(1 + n) (loudness ×1.9 at most), and n ≥ 3 adds a mass thump.
- **Hit events**: `k` 0 hit, 1 status damage over time, 2 skill-zone tick; `s: 1` marks a skill hit. A weapon hit (k 0, no `s`) feeds `A.hit`. A skill hit and a zone tick feed `A.impact` (a skill hit that crits also feeds the crit cue); status damage over time is silent. Damage-over-time hits under 3 damage emit no event.
- **Skill impact**: one cue per hero class per frame, at least 60 ms apart, in the class timbre (arrow thunks, shield thump, fire, bell, blade, bone ticks). The n foes struck set loudness (×0.8 to ×1.6 by log2(1 + n)) and up to 4 layers; a frame with none of your hits plays at half volume and priority 0.
- **Bell cues**: `bstrike` is an FM bronze strike (392 Hz; a Heavy Strike 262 Hz with a low thump) higher and louder with the bell's speed, a chain strike (`c`) a short iron clank; `peal` a long toll (196 Hz: hum, prime, minor-third tierce, nominal, the 2.4 partial and a beating pair over an FM strike and a low boom; the echo 294 Hz at half volume; no beating pair on Low); the chain rattles when a Haul starts and while a bell whirls (faster with speed); `heave` a whoosh, `reel` a ratchet, `bsmash` a crash. Gaps: bstrike 70 ms, rattle 60 ms, peal 80 ms.
- **Deaths**: every `die` event feeds `A.kill`. A flush voices at most 2 deaths per foe type in any 100 ms and 3 per frame, lower and longer for bigger foes (radius); 2 or more deaths in a frame add a body thump and 6 or more a hiss and a sub boom. Kills less than 0.5 s apart form a streak: from 4 a crunch climbs up to an octave (at least 90 ms apart), and streaks of 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 ring a rising chord.
- **Pick feedback** (`src/client/pickfx.js`, `ED.PickFx`): every boon resolves to one card kind (purse > Ascended > duo > class route or fork > its path > general for open boons); relics are their own kind. Each kind has a pick cue (`A.play('pick', v, kind)`, priority 2, under 0.9 s): fire crackle and whoomp, ice glass chime, lightning zap crack, poison bubbling gurgle, holy bell over a choir pad, shadow reverse whoosh, general anvil clang, route horn call, duo its two element cues layered 50 ms apart, Ascended a rising shimmering chord, relic chain rattle and thunk, purse coin cascade; the Hearth buy (`'buy'`) is an anvil strike and an ember whoosh; an in-run shop purchase (`'shop'`, priority 2, 0.45 s) is three coins dropping on a counter and a two-note register clink. Keystones, evolutions and path gates (big) play 15% louder with a held root and fifth. Low quality drops repeated layers. The card click plays the cue (`PickFx.play`); the local pick event plays it only when no click played one in the last 1.5 s. Every hero's pick also bursts on that hero in the world (at most 1.2 s, no screen flash): fire flame column and rising embers, ice shards and a frost ring, lightning a bolt from above and a flash at the hero, poison bubbles rising at their own speeds and popping one after another, drips falling onto a ring that splashes and sinks inward, a low toxic cloud, holy a light pillar and a halo, shadow smoke drawn into the hero, general an anvil stamp (a short impact square at the feet, bright steel shock rings, low sparks bouncing off the floor), route five banner ribbons rising in the class colour and a crest flash over the head, duo both element bursts, Ascended a prismatic burst and a big ring, relic a grey dust ring and a rarity glint over the head, purse coins thrown up. Big picks add 60% particles, 25% longer lives and an outer ring; an ally's burst has 60% of the particles at 45% alpha; Low quality drops the extra sprites. The purse pick emits `gold` and a `boon` event (id `purse`), so the party sees its burst.
- **Fodder**: a non-crit fodder hit counts as `fhit` and a fodder death as `fdie` against the event caps (`net.js`: 12 and 30 per snapshot, 4 and 12 per local frame), so a horde never crowds out crits, threat hits and threat deaths.
- **Score** (`src/client/score.js`): a composed score, no samples. One piece per stratum (58–108 bpm), a camp piece (64 bpm) and a boss variant of every stratum piece (4/4, 100–132 bpm), plus three stingers. A piece is an 8-bar chord progression with a motif that develops across bars, played by six layers (pad, motif, bass, perc, drums, lead). Low quality drops the delay and reverb sends. A track change (camp, stratum entry, boss intro, boss defeated) crossfades; defeat fades the piece out; asking for the playing track again does nothing; the camp piece has no levels.
- **Intensity**: level 0 explore (pad and motif), 1 combat (+ bass, perc), 2 heavy (+ drums, lead); the boss variant starts with bass and perc and brings drums at 1 and the lead at 2. A layer fades in with a time constant of 0.25–0.9 s and a layer at zero schedules no notes; a level change never restarts the piece. `musicLevel` in `src/game.js` runs every 0.1 s on the viewed foe list (host, solo and client alike) and feeds `ED.Audio.setIntensity`: 0 calm, 1 foes alive or the room locked, 2 at 25 live foes (spawning foes and bosses not counted; held until fewer than 12); a boss piece plays its phase (1 → 0, 2 → 1, 3 → 2), lifted to 1 by 8 or more summons. A rise applies at once; a fall takes one step after the request has stayed lower for 2.5 s (from 1) or 3.5 s (from 2).

### Art

- No runtime asset files: art is embedded as data URIs. Vendored third-party packs (CC0 and CC-BY 4.0: 0x72, nijikokun, kosinaz, Ninja Adventure, Puny, DawnLike, LuizMelo, Kenney, o_lobster, Aim studios, ansimuz, CodeManu) live in `art-src/` (licences and hashes in `art-src/SOURCES.md`); `tools/art.js` with `tools/art-manifest.js` crops and packs frames into atlas pages `src/art/{meta,world,actors,bosses,fx,fxbig}.js` (`ED.ART`: pages, frames, anims, palette, font, swaps) deterministically. The UI fonts (Pixel Operator, CC0) live in `art-src/fonts/` and pack into `src/art/fonts.css`. `node tools/art.js --check` fails when `src/art/` (fonts.css included) differs from a rebuild.
- Runtime: `atlas.js` (`ED.Bmp`, `ED.Atlas`: page decode, recolour and rotation caches, pixel font), `actors.js` (hero, foe and boss animations, one default attack animation per class), `pixfx.js` (`ED.Pix` pixel drawing helpers), `tiles.js`, `fx.js` and the per-class `cls-<class>.js` files that register into the `ED.ClassFx` hook registry (`shot[kind]` returns true to replace a projectile body). If the art pages fail to load, drawing falls back to the older procedural code.
- Icons (`icons.js`, boons, relics, upgrades, deeds, paths, statuses): a hand-authored 16 × 16 art px mask per icon (1 px #222222 outline around the body, light / mid / dark / highlight tones from the icon's colour, which is its identity hue, plus a few fixed colours) over a pixel medallion in that colour, nearest-upscaled by a whole factor: 64 or 128 px for the UI, 1× for 16–31 px requests, a glyph-only mask at 2 world px per art px under 16 px. - Hero bodies (0x72): Knight knight_m, Ranger elf_f, Pyromancer wizzard_m, Shade elf_m, Cleric dwarf_m, Necromancer wizzard_f, Bellwarden lizard_f (verdigris scales, bronze belly and belt, crest in the player colour).
