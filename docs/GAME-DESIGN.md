# Wickdeep — Game Design Spec

Canonical rules and numbers. Code follows this file; a balance pass changes numbers here and in code together.

## Pillars

1. **Builds are chosen, not rolled.** Paths gate boons, rooms show their reward before you enter, commitment unlocks a keystone and a weapon evolution, and every hero picks a class route (attack, skill 1, skill 2 or element) with a fork.
2. **Every path is one verb that changes how you play**: Steel = crits, Bleed and the held finisher · Flame = Burn that bursts onto the pack when a burning foe dies · Frost = freeze, then any hit Shatters · Storm = charge until a foe discharges and jumps to the next. Keystones and evolutions change a control or a behaviour, never a plain damage percentage, and none rides the dash. No stratum is weak to or resists an element.
3. **The route is a choice.** Each stratum is a map of branching paths (Slay the Spire style); every door shows the next room and its reward. Dark rooms are a wager: harder fights for richer loot and a stepped-up reward, marked before you choose.
4. **Fights are read, not out-healed.** Low chip damage, heavy telegraphed damage, no sustain by default: nothing heals from hits or kills, and shields are windowed.

## 1. Damage model

- **Depth** `D = World.depth(s, f) = 1 + 5/3 (s − 1) + 0.5 f` (s the stratum I–IV, f the room's row on the floor graph as a fraction of the floor's last row; 0 for the start and the boss): stratum IV sits where the old stratum VI did. Foe HP = base × `1 + 0.65(D−1) + 0.08(D−1)²` × **threat HP** × party multiplier. Foe damage = base × `World.FOE_DMG` 2.15 × `1 + 0.22(D−1) + 0.03(D−1)²` × **threat damage**. Bosses take f = 0. Boss HP is absolute (below), scaled by party and pact. Per-stratum arrays (`World.perStratum`) hold 4 entries.
- **Health unit**: hero health is a small whole number (§5: Shade 46, Necromancer 21). Every foe damage value (`dmg`, `dps`, hazard ticks, boss damage) is in the same points; foe HP and hero damage are on their own scale (`HP_K` 4 converts a hero point to foe HP where a hero's damage taken or absorbed becomes foe damage or healing). Each hit rounds to a whole number, at least 1. Flat health: Vigor +2, Heartstone +4, Grail of Ages +1 per 12 kills (max +8), Philosopher's Stone +1 per 2 relics, Hearth Vitality +2 per rank, mastery 4 +1, mastery 10 +4; max health never falls under 5.
- **Threat** per stratum (`World.THREAT`): HP ×, damage ×, wave budget ×, attack-cooldown rate `cdRate` (foe cooldowns tick faster by this fraction, negative = slower, only while the foe can act; wind-ups never change; bosses are exempt), boss pause × (time between boss attacks):

  Effective foe HP multiplier at f = 0 (depth curve × threat HP): 2.2 / 3.25 / 4.14 / 6.38 (II ≈ old II–III, III ≈ old IV–V, IV ≈ old VI); the last row adds half a depth step. Effective foe damage multiplier: 1 / 1.45 / 2.48 / 4.56. Target: a Knight's mean hit from a regular or elite foe in strata I–II is 3–5% of max health (about 2 of 45); lighter heroes take relatively more; fodder and hazard ticks land the minimum 1 point; a boss blow in stratum I costs a Knight about 2.
- Boss HP: Bone Warden 9,200 · Sporemother 12,600 · Drowned King 17,000 · Hollow King 66,000. Boss base damage 2.4 (Drowned King 3.6, Hollow King 2.9). Champions: Lancer and Hammer 380, Mimic 700, × the stratum's elite HP factor. A foe still alive 45 s after its own spawn (`e.age`; bosses 78 s, except in strata with a boss fury (§8), where bosses are never exposed; fodder never) is **exposed**: ×1.5 damage taken, ×1.5 speed, and it loses 4% of its max HP per second (bosses 1.2%).
- Elite HP × `2.4 + 0.4(D−1)` at the stratum's depth (`World.ELITE`; ×4.4 at IV), damage × 0.9, radius × 1.22. Affixes: swift (×1.4 speed, wind-ups ×0.7 but never under 0.4 s), brutal (×1.4 damage), warded (ward = 45% of max HP), volatile (explodes on death), vampiric (heals 12× the damage it deals a hero: ×3 in the old health unit, times `HP_K` 4). From stratum III an elite rolls a second, different affix (`elite2`) with chance `World.AFFIX2` 45% / 85% (III–IV); HP and damage multipliers apply once, each affix effect once whichever slot holds it (`ED.hasAffix`); the label names both ("Swift Brutal Skeleton Knight").
- Party multiplier (n = other heroes): foe HP `1 + 0.55n + 0.1n(n−1)` (two heroes ×1.55, three ×2.3, four ×3.25), wave budget `1 + 0.6n + 0.2n(n−1)`. A downed hero bleeds out in 18 s when no ally revives them (solo: at once).
- HP-relative statuses (Chill build-up) measure against foe HP before the stratum lever (bosses: `refHp`, the design HP 800–7,800), so inflating HP does not weaken them.
- Contact damage: touching a foe outside an attack state = `0.4 × dmg`, contact cooldown 1.0 s; a hero takes at most one fodder touch per `World.FODDER_TOUCH_CD` 2 s, however many fodder touch it. Attack states (`lunge`, `charge`, `leap`, `bash`, `dive`) = `1.0 × dmg`. Boss contact damage × 0.8.
- Every hit's damage is the same no matter how it was produced; statuses build from **damage dealt, never from hit count**, so attack speed and damage scale them equally.

### Damage dealt by a hero (order, `hitEnemy`)

1. **Hero multiplier** (`max(0.1, dmg + Σ additions)`): the `dmg` stat (`1 + Σ additive sources`), then the additions: conditional rows (execute: foes below 30% HP; lacerate: bleeding; rime: chilled or frozen; galv: charged; opportunist: stunned, frozen or blinded; Brand per ailment; Guardian Light; Embolden) + the class hook (`hitMul`: Momentum, Searing spells, White Flame, Soul Barrage…) + class-element skill hits (`classElMul`), Golden Idol (+1% per 15 party gold, at most 20%), Banner of Unity.
2. **Crit** × `critMult` (1.75 + crit damage; chance capped at 95%), then the class `critMul`.
3. The hit's own factor `src.mul` (Cleave, Ambush, Deadeye by distance flown, Ballista's pierce ramp, Gathering Force, Gangrene, …) and, on a boss, a skill part's `bossMul`; with Frostbite, a skill hit on a frozen foe Shatters (×(1 + `shatter`), the freeze ends).
4. **Foe vulnerability** `1 + Σ`: Marked (Hunter's Mark, `markV`) · Judged or `vulnT` (+0.2, once: Judgment, Hail of Thorns, smoke do not stack) · Frozen +0.25 · Sunder +0.04 per stack (own stacks, max 10). Co-op debuffs are single foe states: the strongest value wins, they never add.
5. `exposed` ×1.5.
6. **Guard**: a foe with `guard {arc, mul}` takes ×mul from attacks inside the arc (damage over time, discharges and explosions ignore it; a Corroded foe has none). Emits `guardhit`.
7. `dmgTakenMul` (default 1). Invulnerable, burrowed or `invulnT` foes take 0.
8. **Ward** (warded elites) absorbs 75% of each hit until it breaks.

Status ticks, Smites, Tolls and discharges are `raw` hits: no hero multiplier, no crit, no further infusion.

### Damage taken by a hero (order, `hurtPlayer`)

1. Untouchable while: hit-invulnerable (0.6 s after a hit, 0.3 s after a hazard), dashing, or inside a skill move's i-frames (`skillIfr`).
2. Mirror Shard (25% chance to shrug off a projectile), then dodge (cap 60%, not against hazards; 0.25 s untouchable after), then Aegis Charm (blocks one hit per 10 s).
3. `amount × 60/(60 + armor) × (1 + vuln)`.
4. **Brace** (Knight, Vanguard) × the best **Sanctuary** here; their product never goes below ×0.5 (`DR_FLOOR`). Then Last Stand ×0.4, then a Judged foe's weakening (Verdict), then the attacker's weakening: Chill (`chillWeaken`), Smother, Grounding and Ember Heart.
5. Rounded, at least 1. A fodder hit is then capped at `FODDER_CAP` (2%) of max HP (a fraction of a point on a low-health hero).
6. The **shield** absorbs first, then health. Thorns strike the attacker; a vampiric elite heals 12× the damage dealt (3 × `HP_K`).

### Shields

- Every shield a hero receives passes one sliding 10 s window in `World.addShield`: **class sources give at most 3% of max HP per window, all sources together at most 6%** (`SHIELD_WIN_CLASS`, `SHIELD_WIN_ALL`). Holy (Smite) and Bone Armor shields count only in the all-sources window.
- Sources: Rallying Charge, Shield Throw catch, Phoenix Mantle, Veil of Night, Judgment kill shield, Sacred Lantern orbs (class window); Smites (Holy); Bone Armor.
- Shields fade by 2% of max HP per second while no room is locked and outside boss rooms (`SHIELD_DECAY`). A source fills the shield only up to its cap: class sources and Bone Armor 8% of max HP (`CLASS_SHIELD_CAP`), Smites `holyCap` (6% base); a shield above its cap is never lowered. A downed or dead hero gains none.

### Healing

- No life steal: hits, kills, damage over time and shield overflow never heal. Heal sources: hearts 22% max HP + 1.5; campfire 35% (once per hero); healing potion 40% (shop); fountain 50% (also raises the downed); Vigor reward 20%; Vigor boon 2; Provisions (Hearth) 2% per rank and Elixir Flask 20% at each stratum after the first; Sanctuary 7% on cast (you and allies within 180); Bone Harvest 3% per minion harvested, at most 6%; revive 35%; Second Wind and Phoenix Feather (once per descent) and Guardian Angel (once per stratum) are one-off saves.
- Healing received × `healMul` = (1 + Heartwood and Elixir bonuses) × pact.
- Hearts drop from kills (`World.HEART_KILL` 2% of non-fodder kills, elites 40%), bosses (two) and breakable props (1%). Cleared rooms drop none.

### Stopping shots, stuns and moves

- Enemy projectiles are stopped only by: the Shield Throw in flight, Shield Orbit, Iron Phalanx's Charge sweep, Bone Knights, and Mirror Shard (chance). No weapon deflects.
- **Hero stuns** (`stunFoe`): bosses are never stunned; elites take half; a stun sets `stunCd = dur + 2 s` and one landing while `stunCd > 0` lasts half as long; repeating sources (waves, shocks, Overload, Shield Orbit contacts, Bone Knight strikes) skip while `stunCd > 0` and set it to at least 1 s.
- **Stagger**: a Heavy weapon's direct hit stuns a non-boss foe 0.2 s (elites 0.1 s), once per 2 s per foe, never while `stunCd > 0`; it does not set `stunCd`. Hit-stop is client render only; the sim never pauses.
- **Parry** (Sworn Blade): a Cleave that starts in the last 0.3 s of a foe's wind-up cancels it and stuns 0.6 s (elites: stun 0.3 s, no cancel; never bosses), once per 2.5 s per hero and 4 s per foe.
- **Dash** (`ED.CONST.DASH`): 0.15 s at 480 px/s (72 px), untouchable for the whole dash. A hero holds `dashCharges = min(2, 1 + dashN)` charges; a spent charge refills after `dashCd` 1.5 s, one charge at a time. Source: Light Step `dashN` +1 (one rank). No path card, keystone or evolution reads the dash. A cap raised by a pick starts a refill timer; the new charge arrives one `dashCd` later.
- Skill moves (Charge, Shadowstep, Comet, Scattershot hop) are no dash; their i-frames last the `iframes` seconds of the move.

**Ember Bolt** (every Pyromancer weapon): each direct weapon hit (a shot, a beam, a lob landing; Afterburn bolts too; never a blast) cuts every spell cooldown by 0.5 × the attack interval ÷ the shots per attack (× 1 + Spellweaver), half on a horde foe; at most 0.8 s per second (a bucket of 0.8 s refilling 0.8 per s), so attack speed, pierce and blasts stop adding past it. A Cinder Arms flame cone tick counts as 0.15 s of bolting each foe in the cone: it cuts 0.5 × 0.15 s (× 1 + Spellweaver, half on a horde foe) from the same bucket, and Kindling stores a Spark per attack interval of channel. The host gathers the cut and sends it every 0.2 s as one `refund` message for every slot (slot −1, whole hundredths). The weapons keep the caster role: low damage per hit, the spells carry about 70% of the damage over a run.

**Flight shapes** of the projectile variants (generic weapon fields, `ED.WEAPONS` header):
- **Beam** (`beam`): an instant line of `speed × life` from the hero, stopped by walls and guards; it strikes every foe on it once (pierces all), in order along the line (Deadeye distance = distance along the beam). A blast or acid lands on the last foe struck, else at the beam's end. A spectral strike adds a 60% hit on the same foe.
- **Lob** (`lob.t`): flies over foes to the aim point (the cursor distance; without one, the nearest foe on the aim line; clamped to 30 … `speed × life`) as a gold telegraph for `lob.t` s; the landing blast is the shot's own weapon hit (may crit, Staggers on Heavy; Deadeye distance = flight distance). Overheat only widens the blast.
- **Return** (`ret`): flies `speed × life` out, then back to the hero; it pierces all and strikes each foe once per leg. A guard or a wall turns it back. A blast triggers on each foe struck. The Shade's Whirlwind ring throws boomerangs.
- **Pool** (`pool`): where the shot ends, a ground pool deals `mul × dmg` per s for `dur` s; a holy pool shields allies (not its owner) by the Sacred Lantern rule (1.5% max health per 1.5 s per ally, 8% cap).
- **Trail** (`trail`): every `every` of flight the shot drops a cloud (`mul × dmg` per s, `dur` s), `n − 1` at most in flight, the last on impact; clouds that touch merge into one. A Grave Bolt keeps laying them.
- Bolt Thrower: each bolt is a Ballista siege bolt. Bone Splinter: each shard can become a Grave Bolt. Dawnbringer waves read `dmg`.
- Sheet model: a beam hits a third of the pack beyond its first foe (`LINE_ALL`); a trail cloud reaches 2/3 of each extra pack foe (`TRAIL_PACK`); a return weapon hits each foe twice when the foe stands inside its range.

Base hero HP: Knight 40 · Ranger 21 · Pyromancer 21 · Shade 37 · Cleric 40 · Necromancer 14 · Bellwarden 36 (every hero below v4.1; armor carries the melee heroes' survival) (armor, the Shade's 35% base dodge and Class Power: §5).

**Bell** (weapon kind `bell`, `src/sim/class-bell.js`): each bell has a state (`ED.Bell.STATES`): Hand 0 (a point mass on a chain anchored at the hero, moving in the hero's frame: carried by the hero's step, its speed is the speed about the hero), Fly 1, Anchor 2 or Reel 3 (in the world frame, never carried). 4 substeps a tick, no RNG. "Weapon dmg" below is the weapon's `dmg`; `rate` is the per-foe lockout of winding strikes.
- **Wind** (attack held, or 1.6 attack intervals after an `atk` action): the chain shortens to its orbit (85% of `chain`) in 0.25 s ÷ haul and the hand bells are driven round the hero (turning sense fixed when the wind starts) at 1700 × haul × (rate gain)^0.6 × (1 + Pendulum) px/s² (rate gain = weapon rate ÷ attack interval) against a drag of 2.5 × haul per second (the same top speed at any haul: a heavy bell spins up slower), at most 900 px/s. **Wind** = speed ÷ (0.9 × the weapon's own top speed), at most 1; a bell at full wind glows. The hero moves ×0.7 while winding (Slipstream: full speed). A wound bell whirls over walls; let go, the chain lets out, the bell trails and walls bounce it (50% of its speed kept; Wrecking Bell 100%).
- **Winding strike**: a hand bell at 140 px/s or more touching a foe (or its wound chain sweeping across a foe closer in, once a revolution) strikes it, each foe once per attack interval per bell, at most 60 strikes a second per hero (a 0.1 s burst): weapon dmg × min(0.6, clamp(speed ÷ 320, 0.4, 1.8)) (Perpetual Motion: 0.9) through the normal hit order, knockback weapon knock × min(1.5, the speed factor). It costs the bell `loss` of its speed (none with Perpetual Motion) and never counts as an attack (no attack count, crit stack, ring, evolution, Masterstroke or proc counter). No whirl guard. A bell at strike speed in hand, in flight or reeled destroys enemy shots on its path and breaks props.
- **Hurl** (a release at wind ≥ 0.5): the most-wound hand bell flies from the hand toward the aim, as far as the cursor (40 to 200 × wind px), at 800 px/s. Every foe on its path takes weapon dmg × k once (k = 2.5 × wind², × (1 + Full Swing) at full wind; knockback × 0.25); where it lands every foe within 40 px (× (1 + area ÷ 2); × 1.2 for a Heavy bell's full-wind hurl; × (1 + Full Swing's width)) not struck on the way takes the same with full knockback. A wall ends the flight (Wrecking Bell: ricochets, 3 times). It then lies anchored. A hurl is a weapon attack: the attack count (Twin Fang), a crit stack, the evolution along its line; a full-wind hurl is a direct weapon hit (a Heavy bell Staggers). Below 0.5 wind a release lets every bell drop and trail.
- **Reel** (a press while a bell lies anchored; a press during the flight reels on landing; holding on after the press winds again): the bell comes home straight at 700 px/s (Swift ×1.3; over walls, never slowed); each foe on its line takes 0.5 × the hurl's k once (no knockback) and non-boss foes are dragged up to 60 px toward the hero. Within the orbit it is back in hand at 0.4 wind. An anchored bell more than 280 px from the hero is dragged after it.
- **Juggle** (2 or 3 bells): each release hurls the most-wound hand bell; the other wound bells keep winding 0.5 s.
- **Snap home**: every bell is back in hand on a room change, a boss phase change and while the hero is downed or dead (world.js calls `ED.CLS[cls].down` each tick). Near an interactable a press interacts and a release never hurls.
- **Resonance** (`p.bellRes`, 0–10): +1 per non-horde foe a hurl or reel strikes, +1 per hurl that struck one; it holds 6 s after its last gain, then loses 1 per 0.5 s; the Peal spends it (§5).
- Heavy and Swift bells fork by their own factors: Heavy lockout ×1.2, damage ×1.32, bell r ×1.2, cost ×0.75, chain ×1.1, haul ×0.85, knock ×1.3 (a full-wind hurl Staggers, its impact 20% wider); Swift lockout ×0.6, damage ×0.66, bell r ×0.85, cost ×1.1, haul ×1.1, knock ×0.5 (Flow while winding, reel ×1.3).
- **Sheet model** (`ED.Bell.cycle`): an attack is one wind → hurl → reel loop of a standing hero (the bell winds from 0.4 wind until full, or until 0.5 wind or more stops growing for 0.3 s, at most 2.5 s; flies to the foe on the orbit, or 150 px into a pack; reels home); a weapon of n bells makes n loops. The hit is the hurl strike; the winding and reel strikes and the route riders are extras. An Anchor Leap's flight (`ED.Bell.busy`, 0.315 s a cast) is time the bell does not wind (`_up`).
- **Sheet calibration** (`sheet.js` `BELL_EFF`): loops a second in a fight over the model's, 0.95 on one foe and 1.15 on a pack of 4 (a hurl reaches `HURL_PACK` 0.9 more foes per extra foe, a reel `REEL_PACK` 0.9; the pack loop is `BELL_MIX` 0.9 of the mix), measured in `tools/sheet-check.js`'s staged chase (the hero holds its spot playing the loop, skeletons walk in).

## 2. Elements and statuses

Six statuses: Burn, Chill (Freeze included), Charge (path statuses), Radiance and Doom (class-only: Cleric, Necromancer) and Bleed (Steel, physical). Elements: `fire`, `ice`, `lightning` (paths Flame, Frost, Storm), `holy`, `shadow` (class engines only). No stratum is weak to or resists an element: every affinity is ×1 (`elemAff`, `ED.elemMul`). Status loops iterate `Object.keys(ED.ELEMENTS)`.

**Infusion**: a path gate sets an infusion stat (`fireInfuse`, `iceInfuse`, `lightningInfuse`). Every direct hero hit (weapon, skills, hold verbs; minions at half strength) applies each owned infusion from the hit's damage: amount = unit × rate × (1 + `statusPow`), the unit being the damage (Burn, Charge, Radiance, Doom) or the share of the foe's HP before the stratum lever (Chill ×340). Holy and Shadow have no gate: class skill parts add rates (`infAdd`) and the Cleric's and Necromancer's Element route sets the weapon or minion rate. Status ticks, Smites, Tolls, discharges, death bursts and explosions flagged `noProc` never infuse. No status, keystone or evolution reads the dash. Verb engine: `src/sim/elements.js`.

### Burn (fire): stacks and the death burst
- One Burn for every source. A hero's **Burn value** `ED.burnValue(st)` = `BURN_K` 0.15 × Power × max(0.1, dmg) × (1 + `burnMul`) per second per stack. `W.addBurn(e, amount, p)` fills the foe's build-up `bacc`; every `BURN_K × Power × dmg × BURN_LIFE` of it becomes one stack of that hero for `BURN_LIFE` 4 s. A foe holds `BURN_CAP` 10 stacks (+ `burnCap`); at the cap the stack that ends first is replaced.
- Every 0.5 s each hero's stacks on a foe deal one Fire hit: the hero's Burn value × the seconds each stack burned since the last tick. Never crits. Stack count (shared by all heroes) is on the foe (snapshot) and drawn on it.
- **Death burst** (Kindle `pyre`): a non-fodder foe that dies holding Burn stacks bursts for the hero holding most stacks on it (chain queue, one hop later): radius (`PYRE_R` 36 + `PYRE_RS` 4 × its stacks) × (1 + `pyreR`); every other foe inside takes the Burn the dead foe still held × `PYRE_K` 0.5 × (1 + `burnMul`) × (1 + `pyreMul`) as Fire (raw, no crit, `noProc`, knock 60) and catches `PYRE_PASS` 0.5 (+ `pyrePass`) of its stacks (`boom` event k `fire`).
- `takeBurn` removes a share of stacks (newest first) and returns what they still held (Rupture, Plaguebearer, Thermal Shock); `copyBurn` copies or moves stacks with their owners and time left (Contagion, the death burst).

### Chill and Freeze (ice)
- `chill += amount × (1 + chillMul) × (boss 0.35 | elite 0.7 | 1)`. Chill holds 1.5 s after the last gain, then drops 25/s. No chill builds while frozen.
- While chilled: move speed × `1 − min(0.45, chill/180)`; wind-ups × `1 + chill/220`; foe damage × `1 − chillWeaken`.
- At chill ≥ `100 × (1 + 0.5 × freezeRes)`: frozen for `1.6 × (1 + freezeDur)` s (elites ×0.75), chill → 0, `freezeRes += 1` (decays 1 per 6 s), `frozenBy` = the hero. A boss is never frozen: a full gauge slows it 40% for 2 s and leaves it **Brittle** `BRITTLE_T` 2 s. Frozen: no movement, AI or attacks; +`FROZEN_VULN` 0.25 (+ `frozenVuln`) damage taken.
- **Shatter** (Frostbite): any direct hit of any hero (not raw, not a tick, not `noProc`) on a foe frozen `FREEZE_SET` 0.2 s deals ×(1 + `shatter`) with the hitter's stats when it has `shatter`, else the freezer's; on a Brittle boss ×(1 + `BRITTLE_K` 0.5 × `shatter`). It ends the freeze, chills foes within `SHATTER_R` 60 by `SHATTER_CHILL` 40 and deals `shatterSplash` of the hit to them (`shatter` event).

### Charge and Discharge (lightning)
- `addCharge`: charge (HP units) held `CHARGE_HOLD` 3 s (+ `chargeHold`) after the last gain, then 40%/s fades. The last hero to add owns it.
- **Threshold**: at `CHARGE_AT` × the foe's max HP (trash 10%, elite 5%, boss 2%) the owner's discharge is queued (chain queue, one hop later): `DIS_K` 2 × charge × (1 + `shockMul` + `capacitor`) as Lightning (raw, no crit, `noProc`); the foe keeps `capKeep` of the charge. Each discharge jumps to the owner's other charged foes within `DIS_JUMP` 120 × (1 + `jumpR`), `DIS_JUMPS` 3 (+ `jumps`) hops deep, discharging them. Arc Flash arcs `ARC_MUL` 0.75 of it to every uncharged foe within 120 and charges them `ARC_CHARGE` 0.25 of that. Bellwarden class discharges use the same queue.

### Radiance and Smite (holy, Cleric only)
- `rad += amount`, never decays. At `SMITE_AT × the foe's real HP` (trash 12%, elite 6%, boss 2%) the owner **Smites**: Holy damage equal to the stored Radiance × (1 + `smiteMul`). Each hero within 160 gains a shield of `smiteShield` (1% base, ×2 from elites and bosses, at most 3%) of max HP, once per 3 s per hero, up to `holyCap` (6% base).

### Doom, Reap and Toll (shadow, Necromancer only)
- `doom += amount × (1 + doomMul) × (elite or boss: 0.7 + doomTough)`. A non-boss foe whose HP (plus ward) falls to its Doom is **Reaped** and dies to the Doom owner. A boss is never Reaped: each time its Doom reaches 5% of its max HP it **Tolls**, taking that Doom as damage.

### Bleed (Steel)
- A crit by a hero with `bleed` (or any hit with `bleedAll`) adds `hit × rate × (1 + statusPow)` to the pool. Drains 20% per 0.5 s, × (1 + `bleedMul`). Physical.

### Ailments and spreads
- A foe's **ailments** (0–9, read as at most 4): Burn (any stack); a Bleed or Charge pool worth 1% of its HP; Chill ≥ 25 or Frozen; Radiance a quarter of the way to a Smite; Doom worth 2% of its HP; Dazed (stunned or blinded); Sealed. The host sends the count. Brand, Open Veins, Contagion and Gangrene read it.
- A **spread** moves statuses: Bleed obeys `SPREAD_CAP` (25% of the receiver's HP); Burn moves as stacks; Frozen is never copied; each foe receives at most once per chain.
- **Chain queue**: payoffs (Smite, Reap, Toll, discharge, death burst) open a chain id; the payoffs they spawn (discharge jumps, spreads, Verdict, Prism) queue behind it 0.08 s per hop, each foe pays each payoff kind at most once per chain id, a trigger hero runs at most 6 entries per tick.
- **Fodder rule**: a fodder death fires no on-death effect (no death burst, no class kill hook); hits on fodder still chain; refunds, shields and stacks never come from fodder.

## 3. Paths

Four paths: Steel (`general`), Flame (`fire`), Frost (`ice`), Storm (`lightning`) (`ED.PATHS`, `ED.PATH_IDS`, `ED.PATH_GATE`). A hero follows at most **2 paths** (`MAX_PATHS`). `affinity(path)` = sum of ranks of the hero's boons in that path (open and cross-path boons count toward none).

- Each path is one **verb**: Steel = the weapon all-in (crits, Bleed, the held finisher) · Flame = Burn that spreads when a burning foe dies · Frost = freeze, then any hit Shatters · Storm = charge until a foe discharges into the next. The gate grants the verb; the **keystone** and the **evolution** each change a control or a behaviour, never a plain damage percentage, and no evolution adds shots to an attack.
- Gate: opens the path and sets its infusion. Keystone (`key`): offered at affinity ≥ 5. Evolution (`evo`): its card is prepended to every level, room and epic offer at affinity ≥ 10 with the path's keystone owned and no evolution yet.
- **Deep cards** (`deep: 2 | 3`): offered only from that stratum. **Cross-path cards** (`cross: [a, b]`, one per pair): offered only to a hero owning both paths; they count toward no affinity.
- **Hold verbs** (`stats.hold`, `St.HOLD`): holding the attack `at` 0.35 s starts the verb (`{t:'hold', on:1}`); attacks stop while it runs; release sends `on:0`. Flame cone (max 2 s, walk ×0.6), storm call (max 2 s, walk ×0.6), ice lance (full 0.6 s after the start), finisher (full 1 s; Masterwork 0.7 s). A release before full fires a weaker lance (≥ 30%) and no finisher. The Bellwarden has no hold verb (its hold winds the bell).
- **Steel melee caps** (`REACH_CAP` 1.6, `ARC_CAP` 200°): a melee swing reaches at most 1.6 × the weapon's range (a slam's radius likewise) and sweeps at most 200° unless the weapon sweeps a full circle. A weapon shock takes only a repeat's own factor, never the Finisher, Ambush or Cleave multiplier. A finisher never Cleaves. Wide Arcs (`wide`) holds 2 ranks.
- **Solids rule**: frozen foes (Glacier) block foes and foe shots; Scorched Earth ground blocks the paths of non-elite, non-boss foes; neither blocks heroes, allies or hero shots.
- **Class element**: Pyromancer = Flame (fire), Bellwarden = Storm (lightning), Cleric = holy (class-only Radiance), Necromancer = shadow (class-only Doom). Their class-skill parts deal that element without a gate. Knight, Ranger and Shade have no class element (their element route is Bleed).
- Rarity: `c` common, `r` rare, `e` epic (keystones, some deep cards), `l` legendary (evolutions). Path colours: Steel `#d9dfe8`, Flame `#ff7a2a`, Frost `#7fe3ff`, Storm `#ffe66b`.

## 4. Offers

- A hero holds a queue `p.offers` of offer specs; `p.picks === p.offers.length`. Offers open only when the room is safe. API: `W.grantOffer(p, spec)`.
- Spec `src`: `start` · `level` · `room` · `class` · `forge` · `epic`.
- Cards: 3 (+1 with Crown of Choice); `epic` 4 (+1). An evolution card is prepended to `level`, `room` and `epic` offers and takes no slot. A draw that finds no card (pool exhausted) shows the Purse.
- **start** (once, at run start; Head Start begins the party at a higher level, so its level offers come at once): the 4 path gates (Steel, Flame, Frost, Storm), for every hero.
- **level**: every even level (2, 4, 6, …). Pool: open boons, non-gate boons of the hero's own paths, the cross-path card of its two paths, path gates while a slot is free, keystones at affinity ≥ 5; deep cards from their stratum. With 2 paths the filler (§3) only fills slots the rest cannot. Weight = rarity weight (`c` 60, `r` 30 + 2.5s + 60·luck, `e` 10 + 2s + 40·luck, other 10) × (own path 2.5) × (gate 0.8); at most one gate per offer. Weapon-kind boons (`pierce`, `split`) need a projectile weapon. **Hygiene**: Opportunist needs a hero that can stun, freeze or blind a foe (`W.canDaze`: a Heavy fork, Knight, Shade, an owned `iceInfuse`, `arcStun`, `waveStun`, `sanctStun`, `cStun`, `chargeStun`, `orbitStun`, `knightStun`, `cometStun` or `parry`, or a stun or blind skill part whose stat is set: Skyfall, Verdict, Afterimage).
- **room** (reward `boon`): the room's `rewardPath` (§6) decides. A path the hero owns, or any path while a slot is free: its gate first when unowned, then that path's cards and its cross cards. A hero holding 2 other paths, or a room with no path: one unowned gate while a slot is free (class element ×2), the rest from the hero's own paths. Then open boons at rarity ≥ r, the filler last. `spec.path` carries the path.
- **epic**: level pool, rarity ≥ r (commons fill the offer when fewer than 4 qualify), 4 cards, keystones ×2. Sources: elite-room reward (25%), boss of stratum III, shop (odd strata), altar.
- **Max rank and the Purse**: a card at its max rank (`ED.maxRank`) is never offered, and `W.pickBoon` grants nothing for one (it still ends the offer). A draw that finds no card (pool exhausted, e.g. two paths with every card maxed) shows a Purse card (`purse`, flagged `purse: true`, in no pool): picking it adds `World.rewardGold(s)` (`round((15 + 8s) × 0.65)`, the gold a gold room reward pays) to the party gold and records no rank, so it has no sheet entry, affinity or deed count.
- **forge**: up to 3 owned boons below max (no evolutions, never route or fork cards: fork ranks come from bosses, §5); picking ranks one up. None → level offer. Sources: normal-room reward (8%), rest-site anvil (odd strata), shop (even strata).
- **class**: the route system (§5): team level 3 (route, `ctier` 1) and boss I (fork, `ctier` 2). Fixed cards, no draw padding, no prepends. A class spec of a hero that already holds a fork becomes a level offer. Fork ranks 2 and 3 are not offers: bosses II and III grant them (§5).
- Offer message to client: `{t:'offer', boons, rerolls, picks, src, path, forge, ctier, route, noRe}`. Rerolls (Q on keyboard, Y on a pad; from Insight, mastery 8 and the Four-Leaf Clover's +1 at each stratum after the first) work on every offer except forge, route and fork picks. In a party a pick untouched for 45 s is taken for the hero: the first non-evolution card, a random card for a route or fork pick.
- **Pacing targets**: see §10.

### Controls and aim

- Defaults (every action has two rebindable slots, keyboard codes or mouse buttons, saved in settings): move WASD / arrows; attack left mouse / J (also interacts); skill 1 Q (rerolls boon cards while they are open); skill 2 E; skill 3 right mouse / R (a hero without a skill 3 casts skill 1 on it; a settings table saved before skill 3 existed moves right mouse from skill 1 to skill 3); dash Space; map and sheet Tab; pause Esc. Hotkeys use `e.code`. Prompts and signs show the live binding (`{action}` tokens).
- **Gamepad** (fixed layout): left stick moves, right stick aims, X or RT attacks, RB skill 1, Y or LT skill 2, B skill 3, A or LB dashes, Back opens the sheet, Start pauses. In menus and the sheet LB/RB switch tabs, B goes back, Y rerolls an offer (cycles heroes in the sheet).
- **Aim is manual.** There is no auto-aim and no aim assist. The aim origin is the projectile spawn point (hero x, y − 4). Attacks and `dir` skills fly toward the cursor; `ground` skills (Rain, Meteor, Sanctuary, Raise Legion) land on the cursor, clamped to range and cut at the first wall (chasms do not stop them; Raise Legion lands on a standable spot); `move` skills (Charge, Shadowstep) travel toward it; `self` skills ignore it. The last angle is kept when the cursor is within 12 px of the origin or outside the window; before the first pointer event the aim follows the walking direction. On a pad the right stick aims (held 0.35 s after release), else the left stick, else the last angle; ground skills reach `range × clamp((push − 0.45) / 0.5, 0.3, 1)` of the right stick's push.
- **Bell weapons**: holding attack (key, mouse or pad) winds, a release hurls toward the aim, a press reels; the client sends the hold as `haulIn` (input `h`), its press count (`hp`) and an interactable in range (`nh`), and the host steps the bells from them (§1, Bell). Anchor Leap aims `dir` (the bell nearest the cursor, else a leap toward it); Peal is `self`.

### Heavy and Swift (attack forks)

A fork replaces the weapon with a derived version (`ED.forkWeapon`, pure), so a re-tune of the base weapon carries over. Nominal damage per second is 1.1 × the base.

### Route system

- Each hero has four routes: **Attack**, **Skill 1**, **Skill 2** and **Element** (the status route: Bleed for Knight, Ranger and Shade; Flame for the Pyromancer, Storm for the Bellwarden, the class-only Holy and Shadow for the Cleric and the Necromancer). A route is a tier-1 card with two tier-2 forks: 12 cards per class, 84 in all (`src/data/classboons.js`).
- **Moments** (every hero, every party member): team level 3 → pick 1 of the 4 route cards; boss of stratum I → pick 1 of the route's 2 forks (rank 1); boss of stratum II → the fork gains rank 2 (a new behaviour: the card's `r2` numbers and `rider2`); boss of stratum III → rank 3 (a transform: the `r3` numbers and the `form`, which renames the card). Ranks 2 and 3 are granted by `W.grantForkRank` with no pick, to every hero including downed and dead ones, and announced with a reveal (message `forkRank {id, rank}` to the hero, event `forkRank` to everyone); the other boss rewards are unchanged. A hero without a fork when a rank falls due banks it (`p.forkDue`) and gets the banked ranks when the fork card is picked. The forge never ranks route or fork cards. A downed hero receives the route and fork picks when back up.
- Route and fork cards never count toward path affinity and appear only in class offers. Card totals: `mods + up × (rank − 1) + r2 (rank ≥ 2) + r3 (rank 3)`. Slot damage (`s1Dmg`, `s2Dmg`) compounds card by card. Co-op cards help allies. Card text on the offer and the sheet is the exact source of every number below.
- Fork tags: Attack forks **Heavy** / **Swift**; skill forks **Burst** (damage) / **Guard** (shields, damage reduction, stuns, slows, blinds, shot swats); element forks **Focus** (single target, bosses) / **Spread** (packs).

## 6. Floors, rooms and rewards

Four strata (`CONST.STRATA` 4), all neutral (no weak or resisted element): I **Ossuary** (spikes), II **Glowmire** (bog: a plain hazard, no status), III **Drowned Sanctum** (water slows), IV **Starless Abyss** (void pools; the finale). Names are plain nouns without "The"; bosses too (Bone Warden, Sporemother, Drowned King, Hollow King).

- **Map** (`Dungeon.generate(stratum, seed)`, pure): a layered graph from the start camp (row 0) up to the boss (row R + 1); R = `Dungeon.ROWS` 15 / 15 / 15 / 6. 7 lanes; 5–6 paths drawn bottom to top, each stepping to lane −1, 0 or +1 of the next row without crossing an edge already drawn; the start camp leads to 3–4 row-1 rooms; every row holds 2–5 rooms (redrawn up to 80 times until it does); the last row leads to the boss.
- **Room types, strata I–III**: row 1 combat; the middle row (`ceil(R / 2)`, row 8) treasure; the last row rest; every other room a weighted draw: combat 43 · dark 18 (from row 3) · elite 25 (from row 5) · event 10 (from row 2) · shop 5 (from row 5) · rest 4 (from row 6, never on row R − 1). Dark, elite, shop and rest never follow their own type on a path; a type a sibling (another child of the same parent) already took weighs × 0.25. A path holds about 9 fights (combat and dark), 2 elites and 4–5 other rooms. A dark node is a combat room flagged `dark` (§7).
- **Stratum IV rows**: combat · combat or event · champion (`champ` 'twins') · rest or shop · champion ('mimic') · rest, then the boss. A row of two or more rooms holds both kinds. A champion room runs `World.startChampion(room)` (bosses.js); its reward is a relic hoard.
- **Doors**: a room's exits are its children, ordered left to right by lane, as north doors `n0`, `n1`, … at `doorX` = (i + 1) × w / (n + 1); a room with n > 1 exits is at least 6n + 5 tiles wide (odd). A map room (row ≥ 1) has a sealed south entry door (`entry`; wall tiles, drawn as a closed gate); the party enters there. Exits open when the room is clear; the party travels together through one exit and cannot go back. Each exit shows the next room's icon and reward; walking up to a north door shows its name ("Elite fight: Relics", "Dark room: Epic boon", "Rest"). `room.threat` pips: a fight 1, +1 elite or champion, +1 dark, +1 past the middle row (row > R / 2; at most 3); the boss 3.
- **Co-op doors**: the party leaves once every living hero (not downed, not dead) stands in the same exit door; downed and dead heroes come along. With 2+ heroes `p.doorWant` (`P[26]`) = 1 + the exit's index (`Dungeon.doorWant`: `n0` → 1); the wait message reads "Waiting for X at the door", and the door preview names who wants it ("Mira wants this door (1/2)").
- **Depth**: a room's row fraction f (`World.rowFrac`: row over the floor's last row; 0 for the start and boss rooms) deepens its foes by `World.ROW_DEPTH` × f depth steps (§1).
- **Room sizes** (tiles of 32 px): combat, elite and champion rooms draw from 33×17, 35×17, 31×19, 37×17, 29×19, 39×15, 33×19 (wider than the 720×420 minimum view, so the camera scrolls); boss 31×19; start, event and rest 15×11; treasure 13×11; shop 17×13; each widened for its exits as above. Interior patterns scale their counts and block sizes by room area / 294.
- **Waves**: `World.WAVES` 4 threat waves per combat or elite room (+1 under Pact 10), each worth `(7 + 2D)` × threat wave × party (D at the room's row), the last × 1.15. From stratum II a room's roster also holds the previous biome's foes (at weight × 0.35) with chance `World.MIX_PREV` 60%. Pacing (`World.PACE`): the first wave 0.5 s after the lock, spawn telegraph 0.7–0.95 s, the next wave when at most 34% of the current wave's threat foes stand (at least 1; gap ≥ 0.9 s) or the current one is 15 s old; foes spawn ≥ 100 px from every hero (turrets 130). Elite rooms: an elite in the first wave and one in the last. Wave advance counts threat foes only (never fodder). **Reinforcement**: the last wave carries one strong foe under a longer, bigger red telegraph (1.6 s, `{t:'reinf'}`, ≥ 160 px from every hero): the elite of an elite room, the affixed elite of a dark room, else the biome roster's costliest foe. The room clears the moment the last wave's threat foes are all dead: the fodder still standing crumble (no kill, drop or on-death effect; pending packs are dropped) and the clear pile pays the xp they owed.
- **Horde** (combat and elite rooms, beside the threat waves): fodder quota `World.FODDER_Q` 28 / 34 / 40 / 46 by stratum × 0.6 in an elite room × `1 + 0.5(n − 1)` per party × 0.6 on stratum I of the tutorial × 1.5 in a dark room (`DARK_FODDER`), shared out over the waves. Fodder comes only with a threat wave: each wave brings its share as packs of at most 8 (`PACK_MAX`), one spawn zone each (r 40, the spawn telegraph) at a door or wall-edge floor point of 2–4 seeded room edges, at least 220 px (`PACK_AVOID`) from every hero (100 px when no edge point is that far). A share is cut so live foes + pending spawns stay within `LIVE_CAP` (60 solo, 90 with 2+ heroes); a cut or unplaced pack is not spawned later.
- **Room xp** (combat and elite rooms): every room drops exactly `World.ROOM_XP` (combat 24 / 50 / 104 / 120, elite 38 / 80 / 166 / 190 by stratum; champion rooms 100 through `roomXp(room, 'champion')`; × `darkLoot()` (§7) in a dark room; not scaled by the party). A typical path (every combat room of strata I–III, a few in IV) reaches about team level 13 on entering the stratum IV boss room (the boss kills add `def.xp × (1 + 0.3(s − 1))`), about 32 boon picks over the run. `FODDER_XP_SHARE` (0.5) of it comes as motes from fodder kills (its share spread over the quota, whole units with the fraction carried). The planned wave foes share the rest: each kill drops its `World.threatXp` (`def.xp × (1 + 0.35(D − 1))` at the stratum's depth, ×3 for an elite) × `enc.xpK`, the factor that makes the planned waves fill that share exactly (fraction carried). Summons and splits in such a room drop none. A pile at the clear pays what the budget still owes. Outside these rooms (bosses, trials) a kill drops its `threatXp`.
- **Rewards** are fixed at generation and shown on doors and the map before entering:
  - normal combat: `boon` 55% · `gold` 15% · `vigor` 10% · `forge` 20%. Each kind gets its share of the floor's combat rooms (rounded by a weighted draw on the remainders). A `boon` reward is claimed by each hero as a room offer (§4); `PATH_BOON` 70% of them name a path (`room.rewardPath`, drawn evenly from `ED.PATH_IDS` on floor gen's own seeded stream, so clients know it without snapshots), shown as "Flame boon" on the door, the Next strip and the map; no room pays a class card.
  - elite: `epic` 25% · else `relic`. Champion: `relic`.
- On clear, a `reward` prop appears at the room centre. Per-hero claims: `boon`, `forge`, `epic`, `vigor` (+2 max HP, heal 20%). Party claims (once): `gold` (`round((15 + 8s) × 0.65)`), `relic` (a relic hoard, one pick per hero).
- **Boss**: 2 hearts and gold. Boss I: the class offer (the fork, §5). Bosses II and III: a fork rank for every hero. Boss II: a relic hoard (rarity ≥ r). Boss III: an `epic` offer per hero. Boss IV ends the run (victory).
- **Breakable props** (`biome.breakable`: I urn, II mushroom, III coral, IV shard): floor gen puts decor of those kinds in `room.breakables` (never baked into the floor) on plain floor off door lanes, ≥ 1.1 tiles apart, ≥ 1.5 tiles from the centre (rooms without a fight: outside the prop band); combat, elite and boss rooms hold 4–8 (topped up from their own RNG stream), other rooms at most 3. A hero hit within 9 px breaks one (melee arc, hero projectile path, team-0 explosions and skill blasts, dash path, Holy lance, Shield Charge, Shield Throw, Shadowstep; damage-over-time zones, minions and beams do not); it drops 1–3 gold 25% of the time (exact, no gold multiplier) and a heart 1% of the time, never xp. `roomState.broken` is the room's bit mask, carried in full snapshots as `BR`; `{t:'smash', i, x, y, k, r}` announces each break.
- **Rest site**: a campfire (heal 35%) and an anvil (forge offer); each hero uses one.
- **Shop**: 2 relics, a healing potion (40%), an epic boon (75 gold) and a forge (`60 + 15s` gold). Relic, epic boon and draught prices scale 12% per stratum; every price is −4% per Merchant's Favor rank, −25% with Merchant's Ledger, ×1.4 under Pact 7, rounded to 5 (minimum 5).
- **Event shrines**: fountain (heals the party 50%, raises the downed), altar (pay 20% max HP for an epic offer), gamble shrine (`35 + 18s` gold for a random relic: 12% epic, 38% rare, 50% common), champion's trial (an elite fight for a rare relic).
- **Relics**: 31, all in the drop pool from the first descent (common 8, rare 12, epic 6, legendary 5). No relic carries an element: the 14 element relics are gone (their path boons are gone with the v7 paths, §3). There is no Codex and no Generalist relic rule. `RELIC_PRICE` c 70 · r 110 · e 160 · l 220. A roll draws at or above the requested rarity from the relics no hero holds (the whole list when every relic is held); weights c 50, r 34 + 3s, e 14 + 3s, l 3 + s. No Hearth upgrade grants a relic; the Stash (§9) starts a descent with gold.
- XP from level L to L+1: `round(30 + 3(L−1) + 6(L−1)²)`. Even levels grant a `level` offer; level 3 also grants the route card.
- Every gold drop (kills, room clears, chests, rewards, bosses) is scaled by ×0.65 (`World.GOLD_MUL`); shop prices are not scaled.

## 7. Dark rooms

- Dark rooms are map nodes (§6, weight 18 from row 3, never two in a row on a path; strata I–III). They are optional: the route a party picks decides how many it enters (often none).
- **Read before entering**: an unvisited dark room carries a violet moon on its door preview and a violet under-bar on its HUD next-room icon and its map node; the preview's line "Dark room: harder fights, richer rewards" fades in as a hero nears; reward icons show the reward after its step up.
- **Harder**: foes deal ×1.3 damage while the room is dark (`World.DARK_DMG`; minions and allies excluded); the last wave gets one more elite with an affix from brutal / vampiric / warded / swift (`World.DARK_AFFIXES`).
- **Richer**: gold and xp dropped in the room × `W.prototype.darkLoot()` = `World.DARK_LOOT` 1.5 × (1 + 0.1 × the highest Deep Wick rank in the party) (`lootMul`, the room's xp budget in `planHorde` and the Tab sheet read it); on clear the reward goes up one step, always (`World.REWARD_UP`: gold → vigor → forge → epic, boon → epic), announced by `{t:'upgrade', to, x, y}`. No extra boon offer.
- Entering an uncleared dark room emits `{t:'dark'}`. The client eases the scene darker while its hero stands in one (ambient +0.3, post-process light level ×0.7).
- There is no lantern, light, oil, flare, dread tier or Gloom.

## 8. Enemies

- Every telegraphed wind-up (lob flights included) lasts `max(t × rate, min(t, 0.4)) × e.windMul`: `rate` 0.7 for swift elites, 1 otherwise; `windMul` default 1. Foes with `e.blind > 0` cannot start attacks. Stunned or frozen foes do nothing.
- **Deeper foes aim ahead and move faster**: an aimed foe shot (shooter, orbiter burst, turret spread, caster wave and homing cast, web) leads its target by `World.LEAD` 0 / 0.25 / 0.6 / 0.85 (strata I–IV, `World.leadAt`) of the hero's measured velocity (`p.lvx/lvy`, eased over ~0.1 s from position changes; host-side for every hero) toward the intercept point (`World.leadAngle`, flight time capped at 1.2 s); the wind-up telegraph shows the led aim. Lobbed globs and fireballs land at the target + velocity × flight × LEAD (cut back at a wall). With D = `World.depth(s)`: foe shots fly × `1 + 0.08(D − 1)` (`shotSpd`), boss shots × `1 + 0.1(D − 1)` (`bossShotSpd`), both × 1.2 under Pact 8. Non-boss, non-fodder ground speed × `1 + 0.04(D − 1)` (`foeSpd`).
- **Ranged foes shoot only on screen**: a non-boss ranged attack (shooter, turret, orbiter burst) starts only while the foe stands within the target's minimum view minus 24 px (`|dx| ≤ 336`, `|dy| ≤ 186`); a kiting foe outside it closes in.
- **Fodder** (`def.fodder`; one per biome, `biome.fodder`: Bone Mite, Sporeling, Brine Polyp, Void Mote): swarm brains; HP `World.fodderHp` = 50 × `1 + 0.3(D − 1)` × pact at the stratum's depth (50 / 75 / 100 / 125; no row, threat lever, party multiplier, elite or exposure): at least 3 attacks of every hero's first weapon at stratum I (2 for a heavy weapon); untelegraphed touch damage capped at 2% of the victim's max HP, one touch per hero per 2 s (§1). No gold, hearts, Grail growth, splits or clouds; every kill counts for the run (`run.kills`, `p.kills`, the summary) and `run.fodderKills` counts the fodder share, while the lifetime kill stat behind the First Blood, Slayer and Legend deeds adds only the kills that are not fodder; a fodder death fires **no on-death effect** (`World.onDeathFx(e)` false: no class kill hook, no Reaper, Spore Burst, Wildfire, Short Circuit, Death Mark or death burst, no corpse, no Death Knell or Killing Edge crit, no Grail growth), while hits on them still chain and explode. Its damage-over-time ticks send no hit event and its hits print no number except the killing blow. It wears an existing foe's art (`def.art`) in its own colours at low detail (no rim light, a 1 px bar once damaged, status marks only for freeze, chill, burn and stun) and rides the snapshot as compact `H` rows `[id, type, x, y, hp/255, flags]`.
- **Crowds slow heroes**: walking speed × `max(0.5, 1 − 0.08n)` (`CONST.CROWD`), n = live non-boss foes overlapping the hero; dashes and skill moves ignore it.
- A wind-up telegraph (zone with `src`) stalls while its caster is frozen or stunned and is cancelled, without hitting, when it dies. Ground bursts placed on their own (lobbed globs, web, boss spikes, spore fields, mire, fire rain) are `detached`: they resolve on their own clock.
- **Caster wind-ups are telegraphed**: a wave cast shows a warn cone (r 230) that follows its led aim for the whole wind-up; ring and homing casts show a warn circle (caster radius + 30) on the caster.
- **No untelegraphed teleports.** When a hero is within 80 and the step is off cooldown (7 s), casters telegraph 0.6 s (marker at origin and destination) and step 150 away.
- Signature foe per biome:
  - I **Bone Priest** (support): raises a dead skeleton (telegraphed), wards an ally.
  - II **Mire Worm** (burrower): travels under ground (untargetable, visible trail), erupts under the hero after a 0.8 s ground telegraph, spore ring.
  - III **Drowned Reaver** (leaper): leaps to the hero's position (landing circle), cleaving cone on landing. Beside it the **Temple Drone** (orbiter): circles the hero at ~160, fires 3-shot bursts.
  - IV **Rift Caller** (summoner): opens a telegraphed rift that spawns 2 starspawn every 6 s. Beside it the **Hollow Sentinel** (guard): frontal shield `guard {arc 2.2, mul 0.2}` blocks from the front, shield-bash charge.
- Bosses use telegraphed attacks in three phases (HP 66% / 33%; the Hollow King 60% / 30%); the pause between attacks is 1.05 / 0.7 / 0.45 s by phase × 0.8 under Pact 5+ × the stratum's boss pause. After a boss falls, hostile shots and pools are removed.
  - **Aimed volley** (bosses whose script keeps it, `opts.volley` not false; the Bone Warden and the Sporemother have none; `World.BOSS_VOL`; an attack of weight 2 in every phase, run beside the main state): a warn line per living, visible hero follows the led aim for 0.5 s × the boss's chill wind-up factor and locks 0.15 s before the first release; then 3 / 4 / 5 shots (phase I / II / III) 0.12 s apart, each led at its release by `World.LEAD`, 0.6 × boss damage, flying `320 + 20(s − 1)` px/s (× 1.2 under Pact 8). Party: one sequence per living hero, 0.15 s apart. Freezing or stunning the boss stalls it; blinding it or a phase change cancels it.
  - **Phase 3 overlap**: in phase 3 a volley also starts every `BOSS_OV_GAP` (5 s) whatever the main attack is, never two at once.
  - **Party**: every summon list gains one entry per living hero beyond the first (at most +3), drawn from the list. Summon guards count threat foes only.
  - **Fury** (stratum IV only; `World.BOSS_FURY` t 95 s, ramp 0.12, max ×4, warn 10 s; strata I–III never): `e.furyT` counts fight seconds from the boss's spawn, through phases, freezes, stuns and blinds. - **Smart policy weights** (`tools/pick.js`): value = sum over the next three strata (weights 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2) of ln damage + λ ln effective health, plus the discounted keystone / evolution value. λ = 2 by default; `--lambda=<x>` sets another number, `--lambda=auto[:x0]` sets λ per stratum to x0 × (foe damage lever of that stratum) / (10% of the mean base hero HP), the lever being the live `World.dmgScale` × `THREAT.dmg` × the mean of a roster foe's and the boss's `dmg`. The policy is the strongest one the bots have, so difficulty targets are read against it; the generic health cards (Vigor, Plating, Evasion, Heartstone, Iron Buckler, Heartwood) are sized so that stacking them does not beat a build of path cards. Read a new policy or card change on `--from=4 --vet` first: wins, keystones and evolved share at IV entry, hero damage over the bare weapon at IV. **Path calibration** `PATH_CAL` (ln-V units on every card of a path) and a weighted start draw (weight exp(gain / 0.04)) are meant to make every path a main path; the four paths are uncalibrated (0).
- **Fight runs** (`--fight=boss|twins|mimic`, `--boss` = `--fight=boss`, with `--from=N`; `--bosstype=<id>` swaps stratum N's boss): each run walks to stratum N (boss: through its rooms into the boss room; twins / mimic: a champion fight in one of its combat rooms), heals the party to full and ends with the fight; the `fight` summary line gives fights, kills, wipes by phase, time to kill, downs and HP lost. Bosses: time to kill 45–90 s (Pacing targets), mid `--vet` deaths ≤ 5% at bosses I–II. Measured (70 fights each, mid, `--seed0=17000`): Bone Warden 45.5 s (0% wipes), Sporemother 44.3 s at 12,000 HP (4%; 12,600 now), Drowned King 55.6 s (13%), Hollow King 75.8 s (46%), twins 29.8 s (21%), mimic 37.0 s (9%).
- **Dark-room policy**: `--dark=smart|always|never` (default smart: the bot enters a dark room while every living hero has at least 60% of max HP, `DARK_HP`); it explores every other room nearest-first. The summary compares dark and normal combat clears (time, HP lost).
- **Verb policy** (`tools/sim.js`): the path verbs (Burn and the death burst, Shatter, threshold discharges) need no input; the bot holds the attack for the hold verb at its skill (the storm call aimed at its target within range). The summary counts death bursts, Shatters and discharges per run by path.
- **Smart picks for the verb cards** (`tools/pick.js`): fold terms from the stat block for every path card (Burn stacks, the death burst, Wildfire, Smother, Ember Heart, the flame cone; Shatter and its splash, freeze control, Cold Cycle, Glacier and the lance; discharges, jumps, Grounding, the storm call; crits on elites and bosses, the finisher), small priors for the cross-path cards, Scorched Earth, Killing Edge, Absolute Zero and the Purse. `PATH_CAL` is 0 for the four paths (not calibrated). Every unmeasured coefficient is marked a guess in the file.
- Power curve (`tools/sim.js` summary lines): damage share by source, weapon share by main path ≥ 20% each, hero damage per second over the bare weapon by stratum, cooldown reduction at run end.
- **Boss-arrival read** (`boss arrival I..VI` summary lines; every run row carries it as `arrive`): on the first entry of each boss room the team level, hero 0's picks by source (start, class, room, epic, forge, level, other) and, per hero at rest, `tools/model.js` `assess` in that stratum's sheet context: damage on one foe, damage in a pack, effective health. Power = (mean of one-foe and pack damage) × effective health, averaged over the heroes per run, geometric mean over the runs (it does not saturate where win rate does). `tools/bal.js --power` prints it per cell with the power ratios smart/random (knowledge gap) and max/smart (Hearth gap), paired by seed.
- **Level pacing** (fresh, smart, `--vet`): team level on entering the boss rooms I–VI 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 11.6 / 13.3, so 6 level offers by the stratum VI boss. Picks in a run come to about 38 by then: start 1, class 2, level 7 (the even levels plus forge offers with nothing to rank up and picks that boons or relics add), room 6, epic 14, forge 7. Dark rooms pay their reward one step up (§7), which is why epic offers outnumber room offers.
- **Open gaps at sign-off**:
  - Fresh random 2.3% (≤ 1%), Pact 3 33.2% (23–31%), three heroes at Pact 5 29.6% (15–25%); half-upgraded Shade 7% (10–30%).
  - Path strength of the four paths is measured only on the sheet (above); no bot batch yet.
  - Fork parity (`tools/sheet-check.js` route bands): the four gates carry their verb, so the mid build (two gates and Focus) is stronger than in v6 (stratum III one foe | pack, v6 → now: Knight 109 | 352 → 171 | 912, Ranger 75 | 106 → 97 | 214, Pyromancer 181 | 411 → 181 | 566, Shade 99 | 337 → 128 | 635, Cleric 140 | 457 → 227 | 872, Necromancer 133 | 199 → 179 | 403, Bellwarden 207 | 452 → 306 | 850) and most forks fall below their +35% / +60% bands.
  - Guard forks: 5 of 12 Guard/Burst HP-lost ratios sit inside 0.60–0.85 (bot noise ±0.15 at n = 20–48).
  - Ranger forks lose 3.5–4.4% max HP per combat room (floor 4.5%).
  - Peak live zones per hero exceed 8 in some Pyromancer and Knight runs.
  - The weapon-arm target (index 1 and 2 weapons) is not measured.

## 11. Interface, renderer and art

### Tab sheet

`Tab` (pad Back) opens the sheet in a run, also while a boon offer is open (an offer does not close it). Solo, the world freezes while it is open; in a party the descent continues. It opens on the last tab used. Q/E (pad LB/RB) or 1–3 switch tabs; Tab or Esc (pad B) closes; in a party a click on the party list or Y (pad) switches the viewed hero. Every number comes from `ED.Sheet.compute` (`src/sim/sheet.js`, pure, identical on host, client and Node) and refreshes live, at most twice a second.

- **Build**: owned element paths (or General) with affinity, keystone and evolution state; the class route tree with forks and next-rank text; every boon at its current rank with full text and the next-rank delta; statuses the build applies with their rules; relics with live values.
- **Combat**: weapon card (damage per hit and crit, attacks per second, reach, extras, damage per second on one foe); a card per skill (effective cooldown, aim, every part with its per-hit damage, radius, duration, count, per cast on one foe, damage per second); dash; offence multipliers each with its sources (damage, attack speed, crit, area, skill cooldown raw → effective, projectiles, a status row per owned element); defence (max HP, armor reduction, dodge, taken multiplier, healing received, current shield (one decimal under 10, as the HUD) and the shield window); foes here hit ×(depth × threat × pact × dark room).
- **Map**: the stratum's map as a vertical node graph (start at the bottom, boss at the top; nodes in whole art px, 2-art-px stepped edges; here, visited, next doors, ahead and out-of-reach states; a violet under-bar on dark rooms, a red one on elite and champion rooms; threat pips on rooms not yet cleared; a legend), the stratum card (row, rules, the current room's doors left to right by name), dark rooms ahead with their damage and loot multipliers, the party (health bars); a click on a party row views that hero (columns reset to the top).
- **Look** (pixel kit): header with the portrait in a well, name and class in Pixel Operator Bold at menu size, weapon and route line, level / health meters in whole art px (shield as a pale segment after health), gold, stratum chips; framed Pixel Operator Bold tabs on the left; three panel columns (Build: paths 26% / boons / relics 22%; Combat: attacks 36% / offence / defence 27%; Map: map / side 36%; minimum widths in text em), each with its own pixel scrollbar. Everything below the header is body text (Pixel Operator). Cards carry the card identity in compact form: element paths and statuses a path-colour edge, the chosen route a brass frame and owned forks a gold frame on a brass connector trunk, open choices a dashed frame, closed ones a dim frame. Fixed anchors, nothing floats:

### Renderer

- The world draws into one small offscreen buffer at 1 buffer px = 1 world px (two alternating buffers); the present step upscales it to the screen (sharp-bilinear in WebGL, nearest on the 2D fallback) and applies the fractional camera offset, so scrolling stays smooth. The screen scale `R.scale` snaps to half steps so one art px (2 world px) is a whole number of screen px; the minimum view is 720 × 420 world px and the snap can show a little more.
- WebGL post (`post.js`): bloom at half the buffer, per-biome colour grade, vignette, grain, chromatic aberration. It is optional: Low quality uses plain 2D, and a software GL context switches to it at a reduced resolution.
- Cached art is a canvas first and swaps to an `ImageBitmap` once ready (`ED.Bmp`); recolours and rotated sprites are cached, never rebuilt per frame. Frame pacing: the loop measures the display rate and presents every n-th frame while frames keep missing vsync (setting Frame cap: Auto, Uncapped, 144, 120 or 60 fps).
- **Wardrobe** (`cosmetics.js`, `ED.Cos`; client only, from each hero's `p.wear`): a skin is the hero sheet recoloured by `swaps['<cls>.<a|b|secret|unkindled>']` (one variant sheet per anim, player slot and skin; portraits, the HUD medallion and lobby portraits too); a weapon sheen recolours the wielded look of that base weapon (forks and Armory variants included) by luminance onto a ramp (first weapon gold, second moonsilver, third violet; never white) and an evolution's outline sheet draws under it; Gilded is the gold ring at the feet; the Hollow Crown sits on the body frame's `anchors.head`; the Platino (`cos.platino` at full size) follows 18 px behind its hero's back with a lag (exponential, 3.5/s), hops while it moves, snaps back after a jump of 240 px, hides while its hero is down or dead and carries a faint light and eye glint for dark rooms; the coin trail stamps a flat gold footprint (3 × 5 art px, no outline or shine, alpha at most 0.5, so it never reads as loot) per 24 px walked (at most 10 a second, left and right in turn, none across a jump or while down, dead, airborne or vanished) that lives 1.6 s and fades over the last 0.6 s, at most 14 per hero (6 on Low); Last Embers replaces the death puff with rising embers; a title rides the name in the HUD hero block and party frames when it fits ("Mira the Contrarian", "Mira, One of Many"; else the bare name) and in the lobby; world nameplates show the bare name. The Blue Hearth turns the camp fire, its light and sparks blue and seats the translucent ghost delver across the fire. The Wardrobe preview (`ED.UI.wardrobePreview`) is a 16:9 canvas of the hero in the worn or hovered set beside a small hearth (its ghost under the Blue Hearth), the pet behind, footprints sliding away while it walks in place, a Last Embers cycle every 3 s and the title under it; its loop runs at 30 fps and stops while the canvas is hidden or the page is.
- **Bells** (`cls-bell.js`, `ED.ClassFx.bell`; render.js queues every bell of a living hero in the y-sorted draw list): a bell's ground spot is the hero's feet + its offset (game.js) and its state sets the drawing. In hand it is drawn 8 px over its ground spot with a shadow, its chain (`fx.chain` links) running from the hand (`Actors.hand`) to the crown, straight while taut, else sagging; at strike speed a ring in the owner's colour and an arc smear about the hero; at full wind it glows. A flying bell rides a paying-out chain with a hurl streak; an anchored one sits mouth down on the ground, its chain slack to the hand; a reeled one drags along the ground on a taut chain, raising dust. Resonance shows as pips round the hero's feet and a patina glow on its bells. The Drowned Bell prop is `weapon.greatbell` at 1.5× in a drowned palette (dark patina, a crack, a glint), rocking on its lip.
- **Bell prediction** (client, `game.js`): snapshots carry each bell hero's row `P[24]` [input seq echoed, ms since the host got it, haul × 100, chain length, flags (1 whirl, 2 hauled, 4 Anchor Leap, 8 winding, 32 turning sense −1, 64 Wrecking Spin), Resonance; then per bell x, y, vx, vy, state] (`N.BELL_HEAD` 6, `N.BELL_N` 5): a hand bell's x, y about the carried spot, any other bell's in the world frame. A client steps its own bells with the same `ED.CLS.bell.tick` (and `ED.CAST.heave` / `peal`) on a stub world without foes (`BELL_W`, `pred`: a hurl spends no attack count or crit stack) at 60 Hz and keeps 1.5 s of history. Each snapshot outside an Anchor Leap is compared with the history at the echoed input's send time plus the host's age: a hand bell turns by 20% of the phase error and scales its speed 20% toward the host's (three snapshots in a row more than 1.6 rad or 60% of the chain off snap to the host); an anchored bell takes the host's spot; a bell in another state than the host's for 2 snapshots takes the host's state when that is in hand or anchored (a flight or a reel runs on its own clock). Resonance is the host's. Strikes and losses the stub cannot see arrive through the corrections. Other heroes' bells interpolate between snapshots (hand bells in polar form about the hero, the others in the world).
- **Playstyle zones and events**: zone kinds `cinder` and `firebreak` are a line (len), an arc band (arc × r) or a disc (cinder: charred blobs with embers; firebreak: charred ground, a solid hot rim, a wall of flames, half on Low); `pillar` a pointed ice column that cracks in its last second; `anchor` a rod with a dashed range ring and crackle (1 arc on Low); `vigil` a gold disc with runes. Events (`N.CAPS` detonate 16, tether 8, rebuke 6, reapchain 8, shatter 8, hurl 8, reel 8): detonate pops (one shared cue per frame), tether flickering arcs (≤ 6 links), rebuke a gold shockwave, Reaper's Chain a violet wisp, shatter an ice burst.
- **Hit feedback** (visual only): a foe flashes for 66 ms on a hit and cannot flash again until 120 ms after that flash ended; damage over time and zone ticks never flash. An ordinary foe's flash is a white silhouette over its body, a boss gets a 50% white overlay on its normal body, and the red wind-up outline shows under both. Damage numbers of one style that land within 150 ms at one spot add into a single number that pops again; a spot keeps at most 3 live numbers (the oldest goes first) and numbers over the same spot stack upward by one text height. A non-crit hit on fodder never starts a number: it only adds into a live one at its spot; a crit on fodder prints its own.

### Audio and music

- **Synthesis** (`src/client/audio.js`): every effect is oscillators and noise, no audio files. Bus gains: master slider ×0.9, music ×0.55, effects ×0.8, then a soft limiter (linear below 0.5, a tanh knee above, never past 0.86). Stingers (victory, boss, defeat) duck the music to 0.3 (defeat 0.2). Settings: master, music and effects sliders 0–100 (defaults 70 / 55 / 80).
- **Voice budget**: every oscillator or noise source is one voice; a cue of priority p spawns only while fewer than 24 / 32 / 40 / 48 voices live (p = 0 / 1 / 2 / 3). Priority 3: hero hurt, down, revive, dark door, room lock, boss roar, boss fury (a rising growl and two heartbeats), victory, defeat. 2: own crits, level, boon, relic, pick, shop. 1: own hits and kills (default). 0: other heroes' hits, foe shots, sparks, xp, gold, spawn. Each cue has a minimum gap (hit 35 ms, crit 50 ms, die 30 ms, boom 60 ms, dash-charge 80 ms, step 100 ms).
- **Per-frame aggregation**: `A.hit(crit, mine, w, el)`, `A.impact(cls, mine, el)` and `A.kill(type, r, mine)` only collect; `A.flush` (once per frame, from `ED.FX`) plays one crit cue, one own-hit cue and one cue for all other heroes' hits (half volume). Body length and pitch follow `w` (the hit's share of the foe's max health, 0.25–1.4); n hits in one frame scale loudness and body by log2(1 + n) (loudness ×1.9 at most), and n ≥ 3 adds a mass thump.
- **Hit events**: `k` 0 hit, 1 status damage over time, 2 skill-zone tick; `s: 1` marks a skill hit. A weapon hit (k 0, no `s`) feeds `A.hit`. A skill hit and a zone tick feed `A.impact` (a skill hit that crits also feeds the crit cue); status damage over time is silent. Damage-over-time hits under 3 damage emit no event.
- **Skill impact**: one cue per hero class per frame, at least 60 ms apart, in the class timbre (arrow thunks, shield thump, fire, bell, blade, bone ticks). The n foes struck set loudness (×0.8 to ×1.6 by log2(1 + n)) and up to 4 layers; a frame with none of your hits plays at half volume and priority 0.
- **Bell cues**: `bstrike` is an FM bronze strike (392 Hz) higher and louder with the bell's speed, a chain strike (`c`) a short iron clank; `bhurl` (a hurl lands, by its k) an impact thump and crash under a bronze clang that drops a few semitones and rings longer with k, plus a low boom and a debris rattle from k 1.5 (not on Low); `reel` a ratchet; `peal` a long toll (196 Hz: hum, prime, minor-third tierce, nominal, the 2.4 partial and a beating pair over an FM strike and a low boom; the echo 294 Hz at half volume; no beating pair on Low); the chain rattles while a bell winds (faster with speed); `heave` a whoosh as a bell leaves the hand and on an Anchor Leap; `bsmash` a crash. Gaps: bstrike 70 ms, rattle 60 ms, peal 80 ms, bhurl 60 ms, heave 150 ms, reel 200 ms.
- **Deaths**: every `die` event feeds `A.kill`. A flush voices at most 2 deaths per foe type in any 100 ms and 3 per frame, lower and longer for bigger foes (radius); 2 or more deaths in a frame add a body thump and 6 or more a hiss and a sub boom. Kills less than 0.5 s apart form a streak: from 4 a crunch climbs up to an octave (at least 90 ms apart), and streaks of 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 ring a rising chord.
- **Pick feedback** (`src/client/pickfx.js`, `ED.PickFx`): every boon resolves to one card kind (purse > class route or fork > its path > general for open boons); relics are their own kind. Each kind has a pick cue (`A.play('pick', v, kind)`, priority 2, under 0.9 s): fire crackle and whoomp, ice glass chime, lightning zap crack, general anvil clang, route horn call, relic chain rattle and thunk, purse coin cascade; the Hearth buy (`'buy'`) is an anvil strike and an ember whoosh; an in-run shop purchase (`'shop'`, priority 2, 0.45 s) is three coins dropping on a counter and a two-note register clink. Keystones, evolutions and path gates (big) play 15% louder with a held root and fifth. Low quality drops repeated layers. The card click plays the cue (`PickFx.play`); the local pick event plays it only when no click played one in the last 1.5 s. Every hero's pick also bursts on that hero in the world (at most 1.2 s, no screen flash): fire flame column and rising embers, ice shards and a frost ring, lightning a bolt from above and a flash at the hero, general an anvil stamp (a short impact square at the feet, bright steel shock rings, low sparks bouncing off the floor), route five banner ribbons rising in the class colour and a crest flash over the head, relic a grey dust ring and a rarity glint over the head, purse coins thrown up. Big picks add 60% particles, 25% longer lives and an outer ring; an ally's burst has 60% of the particles at 45% alpha; Low quality drops the extra sprites. The purse pick emits `gold` and a `boon` event (id `purse`), so the party sees its burst.
- **Fodder**: a non-crit fodder hit counts as `fhit` and a fodder death as `fdie` against the event caps (`net.js`: 12 and 30 per snapshot, 4 and 12 per local frame), so a horde never crowds out crits, threat hits and threat deaths.
- **Score** (`src/client/score.js`): a composed score, no samples. One piece per stratum (58–108 bpm), a camp piece (64 bpm) and a boss variant of every stratum piece (4/4, 100–132 bpm), plus three stingers. A piece is an 8-bar chord progression with a motif that develops across bars, played by six layers (pad, motif, bass, perc, drums, lead). Low quality drops the delay and reverb sends. A track change (camp, stratum entry, boss intro, boss defeated) crossfades; defeat fades the piece out; asking for the playing track again does nothing; the camp piece has no levels.
- **Intensity**: level 0 explore (pad and motif), 1 combat (+ bass, perc), 2 heavy (+ drums, lead); the boss variant starts with bass and perc and brings drums at 1 and the lead at 2. A layer fades in with a time constant of 0.25–0.9 s and a layer at zero schedules no notes; a level change never restarts the piece. `musicLevel` in `src/game.js` runs every 0.1 s on the viewed foe list (host, solo and client alike) and feeds `ED.Audio.setIntensity`: 0 calm, 1 foes alive or the room locked, 2 at 25 live foes (spawning foes and bosses not counted; held until fewer than 12); a boss piece plays its phase (1 → 0, 2 → 1, 3 → 2), lifted to 1 by 8 or more summons. A rise applies at once; a fall takes one step after the request has stayed lower for 2.5 s (from 1) or 3.5 s (from 2).

### Art

- No runtime asset files: art is embedded as data URIs. Vendored third-party packs (CC0 and CC-BY 4.0: 0x72, nijikokun, kosinaz, Ninja Adventure, Puny, DawnLike, LuizMelo, Kenney, o_lobster, Aim studios, ansimuz, CodeManu) live in `art-src/` (licences and hashes in `art-src/SOURCES.md`); `tools/art.js` with `tools/art-manifest.js` crops and packs frames into atlas pages `src/art/{meta,world,actors,bosses,fx,fxbig}.js` (`ED.ART`: pages, frames, anims, palette, font, swaps) deterministically. The UI fonts (Pixel Operator, CC0) live in `art-src/fonts/` and pack into `src/art/fonts.css`. `node tools/art.js --check` fails when `src/art/` (fonts.css included) differs from a rebuild.
- Runtime: `atlas.js` (`ED.Bmp`, `ED.Atlas`: page decode, recolour and rotation caches, pixel font), `actors.js` (hero, foe and boss animations, one default attack animation per class), `pixfx.js` (`ED.Pix` pixel drawing helpers), `tiles.js`, `fx.js` and the per-class `cls-<class>.js` files that register into the `ED.ClassFx` hook registry (`shot[kind]` returns true to replace a projectile body). If the art pages fail to load, drawing falls back to the older procedural code.
- Icons (`icons.js`, boons, relics, upgrades, deeds, paths, statuses): a hand-authored 16 × 16 art px mask per icon (1 px #222222 outline around the body, light / mid / dark / highlight tones from the icon's colour, which is its identity hue, plus a few fixed colours) over a pixel medallion in that colour, nearest-upscaled by a whole factor: 64 or 128 px for the UI, 1× for 16–31 px requests, a glyph-only mask at 2 world px per art px under 16 px. Shown crisp at multiples of 16 device px with smoothing off.
- Hero bodies (0x72): Knight knight_m, Ranger elf_f, Pyromancer wizzard_m, Shade elf_m, Cleric dwarf_m, Necromancer wizzard_f, Bellwarden lizard_f (verdigris scales, bronze belly and belt, crest in the player colour). Each `hero.<cls>.<idle|run|hit>` carries `anchors.hand` and `anchors.head[frame]` (hat seat: bottom centre of a hat, from the first body row at least 7 px wide, plumes and crests ignored). Skins are runtime palette swaps, never extra sprites: `ED.ART.swaps['<cls>.<a|b|secret|unkindled>']` has the shape of `swaps[cls]` (key colours stay the player colour); `unkindled` maps every hero colour by luminance onto one ash ramp.
- Bellwarden weapons (grip = chain eye, mouth down): `weapon.bronzebell` (DawnLike bell, verdigris bronze, patina lip), `weapon.greatbell` (~1.5×), `weapon.handbells` (one small bell, drawn twice); `fx.chain` is one 8 art px chain period, repeated along the chain. Weapon variant tints are runtime.
