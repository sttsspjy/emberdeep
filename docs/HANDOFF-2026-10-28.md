  - Skill 1, pooled win 99% (saturated). Relative final-boss TTK vs the hero's weapon mean: bramble −7 s, wisp −6, blight −5, chainspark −2 (CI excludes 0); bonespear / ricochet / meteorcall +4, censer +4, mortar +3. Hurt/min: chainspark 1.3 (best), censer 6.8 (worst).

Censer hurt/min 25.3 vs chainspark 9.4.
