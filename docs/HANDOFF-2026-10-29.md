## Mechanisms
- Weapon model: `src/sim/weapons.js` header (paths / Forms / refines, `rankPath`, `formsReady`, `takeForm`, `refineForm`, `setRanks`); addWeapon throws on a duplicate id.
- Per hero: `src/heroes/<cls>.js`, `src/client/hfx-<cls>.js`, `tools/check-hero-<cls>.js`, `docs/heroes/<cls>.md` (canonical tree + numbers). hfx-a / hfx-b / wfx-* are gone.
- Q damage = base × levels × might × weapon factor (`ED.Stats.weaponMul`) × (1 + 0.05 (party level − 1)).
- View clamp: `ED.inView` (grid.js) in `hitFoe` and targeting (`w.vclamp`).
- Horde: foe pace ×0.8, roster tiers 0–5 / 5–10 / 10–15 with 6 new foes, benchmark surge at 12:00 (elites every 30 s), XP `10 + 50(L−1) + 1.5(L−1)²`.
- Gold: stage gold ×1 / 1.5 / 2, mid-boss coin, final boss + win gold, run-end kill bounty (1 per 25 kills).
- Render: pixel-true Batch shapes on WebGL and Canvas2D; Form visuals toned for readability; `node tools/browse.js forms [--cls] [--form] [--software]` shoots every Form at full power.
- Yardstick: `node tools/dps.js [cls]` (bands in GAME-DESIGN §4).
