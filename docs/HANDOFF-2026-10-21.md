## Implemented since 2026-10-20 (mechanism)
- **F1 fixes**: stealth last-seen targeting, telegraph stall/cancel, damage-based build-up, fixed 1/60 host step with render interpolation (players excluded), snapshot v5 → v6.
- **Build pacing**: `EVO_AFFINITY` 8, `KEY_AFFINITY` 4, level boon on even levels, fewer epic/forge offers. Median evolution at stratum III.
- **Sustain**: one sliding-window cap `ED.CONST.SUSTAIN_CAP` (0.3% max HP/s) over all hit and kill heals (`W.sustainHeal`); hearts, orbs, campfire, draught and cooldown class heals are outside it. Gloom touch ≤ 25% max HP.
- **Lantern**: first entry to a room costs light (`World.ROOM_COST` 12/17/8, shown on doors and map); slow drain; strata I–III lanterns ×1.3/1.2/1.1. Darkness never adds foe HP: dark tiers add foe damage (×1.1/1.6/2.3), elites, healing loss (×0.7/0.5 at Dark/Lightless), reward upgrades and gold. The Deep stack adds +0.28 foe damage per new room entered at Dark or worse (up to 5, reset per stratum). The Gloom hunts at Dark (8 s) and Lightless (3 s, 1–3 Glooms); it lunges after a telegraph. Flare (F, 6 light) or oil drives it off. Light left at the boss door heals and shields.
- **Balance**: monotone foe and boss HP curves; regular foes die in 2.5–3.8 hits, elites in 4–6 s, rooms in about 14 s. Storm arcs and Conflagration splash cut to 60% on neighbours (`ARC_MUL`, `SPLASH_MUL`). Cooldown reduction soft cap (`Stats.cdrEff`: full to 0.4, half above, cap 0.65). Hemomancy costs 4% HP per rank. Blood gets damage and bleed on hit. Sanctuary heals 7% with 15% damage reduction. Hero HP ×1.08.
- **Bots** (`tools/sim.js`): route around floor hazards; reaction to lunges and flares scales with skill. New summary lines: hits/time to kill, DPS per stratum, damage share, CDR, Gloom share. simpool flag `--explore=minLight`.
- **UI**: menus and HUD share the design tokens in `style.css`. The HUD runs on its own `#hud` canvas at device resolution with fixed anchors. Panels fade when something is under them. Key labels come from the binding table (`ED.Input.ACTIONS`, rebinding in Settings; `ED.Input.hint/fill`), and sim sign strings carry `{action}` tokens.
- **Juice**: the foe hit pose `FX.xform` (lean/squash/pop) is applied around the sprite. Also added: element impact sprays, death ghosts by look family, swing anticipation/smear/follow-through, emissive pickups, layered hit audio.
- **Perf**: baked sprites (`bake`/`sprite`/`blit` in render.js) for telegraphs, statuses, projectiles, bars and labels. Enemy flipbooks (`Sprites.FOE`, 25 looks; bosses and special states draw live). Crowd LOD above 30 foes. Above ~100 Hz the world upload is delayed one frame; `Post.soft` handles software GL. At 2560 High: solo peak 3.7 ms, 4-player peak 6.9 ms, 80 foes 4.0 ms.

1. **Playtest 2 feedback** (user): the first build with fast trash, the dark tiers, the new HUD and juice. Everything below may shift with it.
2. **Fresh profile wins 2%** against the 5–10% target. Fresh deaths are spread over strata III–V (1 / 21 / 70 / 127 / 110 / 47), so there is no single wall. No numeric lever (bosses, elites, hazards, hearts, hero HP, removing the Gloom) moved it by 3 points. 6. **The HUD omits the Deep stack and Gloom count.** Both live only in the host sim, so showing them needs snapshot fields and an `N.VERSION` bump.
