## Implemented since 2026-10-21 (mechanism)
- **Art**: `art-src/` (vendored CC0/CC-BY packs, `SOURCES.md`) → `tools/art.js` + `art-manifest.js` → `src/art/{meta,world,actors,bosses,fx,fxbig}.js` (`ED.ART`: data-URI pages, frames, anims, palette, font, swaps), deterministic, `--check` diffs against a rebuild. `atlas.js`: `ED.Bmp` (canvas → ImageBitmap holders), `ED.Atlas` (page decode, `rec(id)` caches a miss only after the pages decoded, `forget(id)` drops a record, recolour and rotation caches with bakes past the per-frame allowance queued in `rotNeed`, pixel font). `actors.js` draws heroes, foes, bosses and the default attack from atlas anims (procedural path when `ED.Actors.on()` is false). `pixfx.js`: `Pix.define` bakes runtime anims into the atlas (and forgets the id's cached record), `Pix.draw`. Per-class `cls-<hero>.js` register into `ED.ClassFx` (fx.js): marks, `shot[kind]` (returning true replaces the projectile body), zones, events.
- **Renderer**: world drawn into a small buffer at 1 px = 1 world px, present = sharp-bilinear GL quad (nearest on 2D); `R.scale` snaps to half steps; HUD canvas repaints on a value signature; frame pacing `A.pace` with the `frameCap` setting; world text through the atlas pixel font. Hit flash: `hitEnemy` sets `e.flash` (66 ms) unless the hit is `dot`, and only when the last flash ended 120 ms ago (`flash` starts at −1); ordinary foes draw a white body, bosses a 50% white overlay, the red wind-up outline is always drawn (`actors.js`). Damage numbers: `F.text` with `sum` adds into a same-style number of the same spot younger than 150 ms and keeps at most 3 per spot; other texts stack by font height. `F.addK` scales additive pops and sprites (0.55 on Rimefrost). `ED.Actors.reset()` clears per-entity state at each session start. The boss bar is a fading HUD panel.
- **Aim**: game.js `aimOf` (cursor; pad right stick latched 0.35 s); `Sim.groundPoint` is shared by the preview, the send and the host check; `ED.AimFx` (aim.js) draws the cursor and previews; `ED.SKILLS[id].aim` is `dir | ground | move | self`; no aim assist anywhere. Settings: `cursor`, `reticle`, `reticleCol`, `aimPreview`, `frameCap`, `sheetTab`.
- **Skills**: `ED.SKILLS` parts (`base + pow × P`, flags `if/ifNot/of/mul/times/per`) evaluated by `Stats.skillNums` (cached on the stat block) are the one source for sim, sheet and bots. `ED.CAST[skill]` in `class-<hero>.js`; `ED.CLS[cls]` hook tables (listed at the top of skills.js). Cooldowns stay client-authoritative; `refundSkill` sends `refund` messages.
- **Routes**: `classboons.js` (72 cards: `route`, `tier`, `forks`, `mods`/`up`/`r3`, `weapon`, `rider`) merged into `ED.BOONS`; totals through `ED.cardTotals`, text through `ED.cardParts` tokens; `Stats.sources/compute` fold cards into the stat block (slot damage compounds). Class offers in `build.js` `rollOffer('class')` (ctier 1 at team level 3, 2 at boss I, 3 at bosses II and IV); `ED.forkWeapon` derives Heavy and Swift from the base weapon (pure, memoized).
- **Paths**: `ED.PATHS`, `ED.ELEMENTS` (infusion stat, adder, unit), `ED.STATUS` (rules for the sheet). combat.js adders per status, `smite`, `checkReap`/`checkToll`, chain queue (`openChain`, `queuePayoff`, `updateChains`, `runPayoff`), `moveStatuses`, `ailments`, `stunFoe`, `parry`, `grantCrits`; `ED.COND_DMG` rows shared with the sheet. Offers: one gate per slot, coverage weights, duo and evolution prepended.
- **Defence**: `World.addShield` sliding window (`p.shWin`, `SHIELD_WIN_*`, `p.shieldBy`, `p.shieldClip`), `updateShields` decay, no heal overflow, no lifesteal; `hurtPlayer` order in GAME-DESIGN §1.
- **Sheet**: `sim/sheet.js` (`ED.Sheet.compute`, `Sh.text`, `Sh.nextText`) is pure and identical on host, client and Node; `client/sheetui.js` renders three tabs; `Stats.sources` gives per-stat source chips.
- **Save and net**: save.js count-prefixed lists (v4), profile `v` 2 and `news` flag, `UI.maybeNews`; per-hero `maxRelics`/`revivesGiven` in the run summary. net.js snapshot layout (hero flags 0–12 and 20, P row, foe flags) is documented in its header comments; `N.CAPS` drops excess fx events per type.
- **Tools**: bot flags `--route --fork --weapon --start --gates --force --mastery --aimnoise --out --pair`; `mid`/`max` presets carry Codex relics (old `mid` baselines no longer compare). Checks: `check-sim`, `check-classes`, `check-ranger`, `check-pyro`, `check-shade`, `check-cleric`, `check-necro`, `check-save`, `check-ux`, `check-render` (hit-flash rules, art anchors), `sheet-check`, `art --check`; `simpool.js` runs at normal priority, `--nice` opts into idle priority; `tools/sheet.js` gives analytic hero numbers in milliseconds before bot hours.

- **Perf** (`cd1074d`, one RTX 4070 SUPER, 240 Hz, dpr 1): Firefox 150 and Zen 158 hold 240 fps in crowd70, solo8 and real play (v2 142–208); main-thread time per frame 2.0–2.2 ms with 70 foes (v2 3.6–3.7); the draw sources per frame are about 1.6 canvases plus ImageBitmaps (v2 138 canvases). With hardware acceleration off, Gecko runs at 150–231 fps (v2 22–38). Chromium runs at 230–240 fps, missing about one vsync in 20 because the GPU downclocks under light load (bare canvas pages show the same). Unfocused Playwright windows are throttled, so a bench must bring its window to the front.

## Remaining tasks (priority, reason)
1. **Fresh-profile difficulty**: fresh smart wins 1% (target 5–10%), fresh depth 4.6 (target 2.5–3.5). Reason: a veteran must be able to win fresh (decision 2026-10-20); it needs a lever, then the two Fresh rows again. The three-hero row (42%, target 45–70%) is the only other row below target.
2. **Guard-band precision**: 5 of 12 Guard/Burst HP-lost ratios sit inside 0.60–0.85 and bot noise is ±0.15 at n = 20–48; about 60 runs per cell or `--routes` arms are needed before any retune. Reason: current data cannot separate noise from fault.
3. **Ranger intake floor**: Ranger forks lose 3.5–4.4% max HP per combat room (floor 4.5%). Reason: "no sustain by default" target.
4. **Zone budget**: peak live zones per hero above 8 in some Pyromancer and Knight runs. Reason: perf cap in §10.
5. **Weapon arm**: the target for index 1 and 2 weapons (`--weapon=rotate`) is not measured. Reason: the ±15% weapon-versus-class-median target is untested for them.
6. **Share-metric definition**: decide whether route and weapon shares count direct damage only or all damage, then align `sim.js`, `sheet.js` and §10. Reason: the ≥ 8 and ≥ 6 point route targets read differently by definition.
7. **`sheet.js` model gaps**: Bone Knight ×2, Shield Orbit stun, Flurry and Scattershot slow are not modelled. Reason: the sheet and `tools/sheet.js` omit those effects.
8. **Dead guard**: `src.kind === 'minionBurst'` in `class-necro.js` and `combat.js` has no emitter. Reason: remove or wire it.
9. **Soul Command with two Necromancers** shows no command stream (`cls-necro.js`; the wire has no Hex owner). Reason: co-op readability.
10. **Deeds** are a 31-bit mask in profile storage and decode (`1 << i`, load clamps to 2^31 − 1; 26 used): a 32nd deed resets every deed on reload and needs new profile storage. The save code's 6-bit deed count holds 63, so no `CODE_VERSION` bump.
11. **Party clients** get no aim banner on their first descent; only the solo tutorial signs explain mouse aim.
12. **Tooling**: a killed `simpool.js` parent has left orphaned `--worker` processes (not re-checked since the priority change to normal).
