## Implemented (mechanism)
- **Bellwarden** (`bell`, last in CLASS_IDS, element lightning, hp 44, armor 30, P 18):
  - Physics in `class-bell.js`: point-mass bells on a chain, moving in the hero's frame. Haul (attack held) drives the spin and shortens the chain to its orbit (≤ 30 px).
  - Strikes: weapon dmg × clamp(speed/320, 0.4, 1.8). The hauled chain also strikes foes it sweeps across. Whirl guard: −45% damage taken while hauling at speed.
  - Skills: Heave (fling, reel; the bells keep their speed) and Peal (stops the bells, wave r85 scaled by bell speed).
  - Forks use their own factors (`BELL_FORK`).
  - Sheet: `ED.Bell.busy` for weapon uptime and `BELL_EFF` calibration, checked against a staged chase in sheet-check.
  - Bot: orbit and haul logic in `tools/sim.js`.
- **Bell client**:
  - `cls-bell.js` draws the bells, chain and effects. `ClassFx.bellIdle` draws a resting bell for the camp, preview and lobby.
  - Snapshot row `P[24]` (`N.bellRow`). Input carries `h` (haul held) and `q` (seq).
  - Own-bell prediction steps the same `ED.CLS.bell.tick` on the stub `BELL_W` in `game.js` and reconciles 20% per snapshot. A world call added to class-bell.js that runs without foes needs a no-op on `BELL_W`.
- **Unlock**:
  - Drowned Bell prop (`room.bell`) in the first dark-pick room of stratum V. Ringing it and staying ≥ 50% HP until the clear sets `p.sec.bellRung`.
  - A victory then sets `profile.bellUnlocked`. While locked, the menus show a silhouette named "???" with the hint only.
- **Secrets**:
  - `ED.SECRETS` (15, positional bits in `profile.secrets`). Sim fields are `p.sec` (platino, contrarian, thread, heroSecret, bellRung) plus `run.gold777` and `run.oneFace`.
  - Clients get them through the RUN row `pl`. Event `{t:'secret'}` shows a nameless toast. `S.checkSecrets` runs in `S.applyRun`.
  - Hearthsitter is client-only: 180 s idle on the Hearth screen (`main.js`).
  - Platino critter: one floor in 8, in a cleared room, on its own RNG stream; it never touches `w.rng`.
- **Wardrobe**:
  - `ED.COSMETICS` scopes (global, then per hero, append-only). Profile fields `cos`, `evo`, `heroWins`, `wear`.
  - Purchases are non-refundable and outside Reset all.
  - Rendering lives in `cosmetics.js` (`ED.Cos`). Skins are runtime swaps `ED.ART.swaps['<cls>.<skin>']`.
  - Co-op: `S.wearBundle` rides hello, pick and start; `N.cleanWear` sanitises it (ownership is not verified).
