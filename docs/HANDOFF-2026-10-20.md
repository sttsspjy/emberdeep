## Implemented (mechanism)
- Paths: 6 paths gate boons (max 3 gates per hero), keystone at affinity 3, evolution at affinity 5 + keystone, duo boons; offers are queued by source (level/room/class/start/forge/epic). `src/sim/build.js`, `src/data/boons.js`.
- Elements: build-up proportional to damage (burn pool → combust, chill → freeze with resistance, charge → discharge arcs, bleed). Every stratum is weak to one element and resists another (`biomes.js` weak/resist). `src/sim/combat.js`.
- Damage: contact 0.4× except in lunge/charge/leap/bash/dive states; no passive projectile deflect; leech only in Blood path, capped at 1.2% max HP/s.
- Skill 2 (L/C/E/middle mouse, pad Y/LT) per hero in `src/sim/skills.js`; Knight/Ranger/Pyro start unlocked.
- Floors 12–14 rooms with fixed rewards shown on doors and the map, elite and rest rooms. Lantern light drains per stratum; Dread tiers (Bright/Dim/Dark/Lightless) raise foe stats and gold; Gloom stalker at Lightless. `src/sim/dungeon.js`, `world.js`, `rooms.js`.
- 7 new foes, telegraphed caster steps, new boss attacks, 3 boss phases. `src/data/enemies.js`, `src/sim/ai.js`, `bosses.js`.
- Visuals: WebGL post-process (`src/client/post.js`: bloom, grade, vignette, grain) over a coloured light buffer, 2D fallback on Low quality.
- Balance (W7): per-stratum `World.THREAT` table, boss HP, XP curve, gold ×0.65, lantern drain, Hearth steps. Numbers in GAME-DESIGN §1, §6, §7, §10. Bots (smart picks, 384 runs): fresh 1%, mid 31%, max 71%, max Pact 5 24%, parties 58/64%.
- Snapshot `N.VERSION = 4`; save codes `WD1-`, `CODE_VERSION 2`.

### F2: design and balance (after F1; one balance Worker at a time, since it uses the whole CPU)

**R1. Sustain (point 5)**
- Problems:
  - Sanguine Arms `steal: 0.15` heals uncapped (combat.js ~650). It is 22% of all healing and worth about 12 win points.
  - Blessed Strikes heals per foe struck, uncapped.
  - Leech is still worth +11 to +17 win points.
  - Bots heal 1.7–2.6× the damage they take.
- Fix: one shared per-second sustain cap of about 0.5% max HP/s covering leech, steal and blessed. Check Judgment's kill-heal too (unverified).
- Accept when `--force=bloodpact,leech` adds at most 5 win points.

**R4. Build pacing (points 4 and 7)**
- Problems:
  - Evolutions land at stratum I–III, hero level 3–9.
  - About 44 offers per run (46 boons) fill all 3 paths, so committing costs nothing.
- Fix:
  - Raise EVO_AFFINITY to about 8 and KEY_AFFINITY to 4–5, or make evolution a boss reward from stratum III on.
  - Halve epic and forge offers, and give a level boon only every other level.

**R5. Lantern (point 6)**
- Problems:
  - Win rate rises with rooms explored: 10%, 23%, 35%, 33% at 54%, 70%, 87%, 98% of rooms. Exploring is free, and the per-second drain taxes slow builds.
  - In a party the lantern drains while heroes pick boons (game.js ~387).
- Fix:
  - A per-room entry cost, shown on doors and the map, plus a small per-second drain.
  - Steeper Dim tier (damage ×1.1).
  - A reward for unspent light at the boss door.
  - Pause the drain during party offers in safe rooms.
- Accept when the win rate in the explore sweep peaks at 70–80% of rooms visited.

**R6. Gloom**
- The Gloom is the top killer in the max profile (6 of 9 first downs), and running away is the only counterplay.
- Options: drain light instead of HP, cap its damage, let oil dispel it, or lengthen its telegraph.

**R7. Power curve (after R1–R5)**
- Problems:
  - Effective trash HP by stratum is ×3 / 5.2 / 7.9 / 5.9 / 19.5 / 15.9; boss HP is 6–19× refHp.
  - Late DPS is 30–55× the bare weapon. Weapons deal 8–30% of late damage, while chain/Storm deals 57–64%.
  - Builds reach 0.48–0.68 cooldown reduction, often overshooting the 0.65 cap.
  - Hemomancy costs a flat 4% current HP per cast while its bonus grows +35% skill damage per rank.
- Fix:
  - Add a per-hero DPS-per-stratum metric to tools/sim.js.
  - Find the cause of the IV→V spike.
  - Re-fit a monotone HP curve for trash and bosses.
  - Tone down Storm arcs.
  - Soft-cap cooldown reduction.
  - Scale the Hemomancy cost with rank.
