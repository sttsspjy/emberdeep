## Implemented (mechanism)
- Paths: 6 paths gate boons (max 3 gates per hero), keystone at affinity 3, evolution at affinity 5 + keystone, duo boons; offers are queued by source (level/room/class/start/forge/epic). `src/sim/build.js`, `src/data/boons.js`.
- Elements: build-up proportional to damage (burn pool → combust, chill → freeze with resistance, charge → discharge arcs, bleed). Every stratum is weak to one element and resists another (`biomes.js` weak/resist). `src/sim/combat.js`.
- Damage: contact 0.4× except in lunge/charge/leap/bash/dive states; no passive projectile deflect; leech only in Blood path, capped at 1.2% max HP/s.
- Skill 2 (L/C/E/middle mouse, pad Y/LT) per hero in `src/sim/skills.js`; Knight/Ranger/Pyro start unlocked.
- Floors 12–14 rooms with fixed rewards shown on doors and the map, elite and rest rooms. Lantern light drains per stratum; Dread tiers (Bright/Dim/Dark/Lightless) raise foe stats and gold; Gloom stalker at Lightless. `src/sim/dungeon.js`, `world.js`, `rooms.js`.
- 7 new foes, telegraphed caster steps, new boss attacks, 3 boss phases. `src/data/enemies.js`, `src/sim/ai.js`, `bosses.js`.
- Visuals: WebGL post-process (`src/client/post.js`: bloom, grade, vignette, grain) over a coloured light buffer, 2D fallback on Low quality.
- Balance (W7): per-stratum `World.THREAT` table, boss HP, XP curve, gold ×0.65, lantern drain, Hearth steps. Numbers in GAME-DESIGN §1, §6, §7, §10.
