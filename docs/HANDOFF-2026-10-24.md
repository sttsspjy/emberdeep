## Implemented (mechanism)
- **Melee swing** (`actors.js`):
  - `swing()` is a two-ended timeline: wind-up past the last end, strike to the opposite end, overshoot, hold 0.7 s, ease to idle over 0.35 s. Thrusts poke and retract; spins end where they began.
  - The blade hangs from a shoulder anchor through a pixel arm (`armAt`). It draws behind the body when `behindBody`. Arc melee blades rest `IDLE_LOW` 0.75 rad below the aim.
  - `browse.js --scen=swing` writes contact sheets.
- **Health ÷4** (`ED.CONST.HP_K` 4):
  - Hero hp: Knight 45, Ranger 29, Pyro 27, Shade 41, Cleric 44, Necro 23. Enemy dmg ×0.25.
  - Heals and shields that read HP are scaled by `HP_K`.
  - World.THREAT damage for strata I–III is 1/1/1; bosses I–III base dmg 2.4. A regular hit on a Knight at I–III is about 2 (3.5% of max health).
  - The max-health floor is 5. The HUD and the sheet show fractional HP rounded up (shields one decimal under 10).
  - Hearth Vitality and mastery 4 stay flat +1 (user: legibility).
- **Armory** (`ED.ARMORY` in `meta.js`, entries in `classes.js` WEAPONS with `variant`):
  - 18 variants, one per base weapon, cost `40 + 20 × base index`. Owned once the base is unlocked; an owned variant is its own weapon-picker choice (`ED.weaponPick`, `ED.weaponChoices`; `net.js cleanPick` validates).
  - Primitives in `combat.js`: `combo` steps (`St.stepWeapon`, the atk action carries `k`), `ahead`, `pull`, `ring` (no wave), `beam` (`fireBeam`), `lob` (`lobShot`, lands on the cursor distance `d`, else on the nearest foe on the aim line), `ret` (return flight), `pool` (`allyPool` shields), `trail`.
  - Same-power gate: sim mean of the one-foe and pack-4 dps within ±10% of the base. `tools/check-armory.js` and `tools/check-armory-b.js` cover it.
- **News** (`save.js`, `ui.js`):
  - `news` 1 shows the full notice; 3 shows only `NEWEST` (health, Armory, melee).
  - An era-3 profile or a v5 code with runs gets 3. `CODE_VERSION` 6 has the layout of 5.
- **Resist** (another session, user-approved): a resisted element is ÷1.5 instead of ×0.5.
- **Pixel UI** (game-ui; details in GAME-DESIGN §11):
  - **Fonts**: Pixel Operator (CC0) is embedded via `tools/art.js` → `src/art/fonts.css`; there are no Google Fonts.
  - **Grid**: `ED.PixUI` uses k = floor(min(W·dpr/480, H·dpr/270)) device px per art px.
  - **Frames**: ASCII masks are baked at boot (`uiart.js`, `screenart.js`, `cardart.js`, `sheetui.js`).
  - **Cards and pick feedback**: 13 card identities (`ED.PickFx.kind`), with a pick animation, cue and hero burst (`pickfx.js`) on the `boon`/`relic` events. The Purse emits `boon`.
  - **HUD text**: rasterised through `ED.HUD.pixText`.
  - **Tests**: `browse.js` scenarios `cards`, `picks`, `screens`, `sheet`.

## Remaining tasks (priority, reason)
1. **Hearth rule** (+21.6 against +6.75 allowed), from a worker ablation on 120 paired runs:
   - Health (Vitality + mastery 4) is the largest single term, about 5 win points. Offense stats are 2.5, economy 1.7, Bulwark and rerolls 0.8 each, Second Wind 0.
   - Meeting the rule means halving every stat step: Might, Precision, Alacrity, Bulwark, mastery damage/crit/recharge, Wisdom and Deep Wick.
   - Validate with `bal.js --power` (cheap, low noise) before any win batch.
   - Health stays flat +1 per the user. Percent health rounds to +0 at rank 1.
2. **Random picks 3.4%** (target ≤ 1%). The resist ÷1.5 change doubled it and shrank the knowledge gap.
3. **Second Wind is dead weight**: about 97% of bot deaths are at bosses, where it never fires.
4. **UI not verified**:
   - dpr ≠ 1;
   - gamepad menu navigation;
   - phone width under 560 px;
   - Firefox visuals beyond smoke;
   - route bursts for non-Knight heroes;
   - real party frames.
   - Small issues: a 60 ms white hero flash on each pick; at 800×600 frames are 1 px; the sheet map's icons are large at 1080p.
5. **Armory polish**:
   - Variant looks are tints only (the Arming Sword reads pale).
   - `sheet.js --weapons` shows Votive +17%, but the sim says −2.5% (the base lantern's PIERCE_LINE pack term).
   - No bot batch has used variants: the bots wield base weapons.
6. **Health-scale leftovers**: 3% fodder caps and shields sit under the 1-point minimum hit, so HP is often fractional.
