## Implemented since 2026-10-22 (mechanism)
- **Testing**:
  - `tools/bal.js` is the fixed quick suite (every cell `--vet`, bands from the difficulty contract, `--full` sign-off sizes, `--power` boss-arrival power, `--runs`).
  - `--from=N` stage start: `skipStrata` plays strata 1..N−1 without fighting through the `nextRoom` policy.
  - `--vet` is the human-proxy bot.
  - `--lambda` sets the EHP weight of the smart pick.
  - `tools/pick.js` is the smart card scorer; `tools/model.js` is the shared damage/EHP model.
  - `tools/sheet.js --parity [--strict]` checks the fork bands.
  - `tools/browse.js` is headless Playwright (smoke, crowd70, shots; one browser, never visible).
  - `check-perf` has interleaved reps, a load-calibrated absolute gate, a cost(200)/cost(70) ≤ 4.5 ratio gate and one retry.
- **Encounters (88)**:
  - Spatial foe grid: `foeScan`/`foeTail`/`foeEnd`.
  - Rooms about 2× area with a scrolling camera.
  - Fodder: `e.fodder`, which drops no heart or gold, gives XP motes summing to the room budget, and never feeds refunds, shields or stacks. Fodder travels in snapshot H rows; capFx keys `fhit`/`fdie`.
  - Dark rooms (`W.DARK_*`) replace the lantern.
  - Crowd slow.
  - Dash charges: `st.dashCharges`, `st.dashCd`.
  - Lead aim (`World.LEAD`).
  - Boss aimed volleys (`BOSS_VOL`) and the phase 3 overlap.
  - Breakables: `room.breakables`, `smash` event.
  - Boss fury IV–VI (`World.BOSS_FURY`, `furyT` in E[19]). Fury-strata bosses are never exposed.
  - IV–VI calibration: boss HP, damage and pause, THREAT damage, and fury t ≈ 1.05× the smart median boss TTK. Fury is the knowledge check: slow (random) builds reach it, smart builds mostly do not.
- **Build (6a)**:
  - Card engine: `mods + up×(r−1) + r2 + r3`, `rider2`, `form`, `ED.cardName`/`cardParts`/`cardTotals`.
  - `W.grantForkRank` gives rank 2 at boss II and rank 3 at boss IV; `p.forkDue` banks ranks.
  - Ascended boons (rar `a`, `src/data/ascended.js`, multiplicative bucket) from IV.
  - Attack and element forms for all six heroes.
  - Codex removed; element relics are now path boons.
  - Hearth: free refunds and Reset all, small Hearth.
  - Armory shell (`ED.ARMORY` empty).
  - Save v5.
  - Second Wind revives only outside boss fights.
  - An exhausted offer pool shows a Purse card (gold); maxed cards are never offered.
  - Hero bases were scaled for boss TTK parity (Knight ×1.30, Cleric ×1.35, Necro ×1.5 weapon dmg, Power and skill bases; Ranger weapons ×0.92).
  - Melee EHP: Knight 178 HP / 32 armor, Cleric 175 / 14, Shade 165 HP with 35% base dodge (class `dodge` mod).
  - Generic HP cards trimmed about 40%.
- **Skills (f1)**:
  - Rank 2 `rider2` and rank 3 `form` for all 24 skill forks; forks inside the parity bands.
  - Boss-only factors: a part's `bossMul` is copied by `W.partSrc` and applied in `hitEnemy`. Values: Shield Charge impact ×3, Shield Throw strike ×2, Judgment legs ×2, Avalanche slam ×1.5.
  - The Cleric form stat is `dawn`; `aegis` is the Aegis Charm relic.
  - Shadowstep leaves the Shade untouchable for `STEP_GRACE` 0.35 s after landing.
  - Fork parity (`--parity --strict`, all 48 forks): IV r2 non-Guard 31.5–38.5 and Guard ≥ 28; VI r3 non-Guard 54–66 and Guard ≥ 48, plus EHP ≥ +25% or control ≥ 30%.
- **Audio (f1)**:
  - `audio.js`: voice budget by priority, per-frame aggregation (`A.hit`/`A.impact`/`A.kill`, `A.flush`), skill impact cues, kill streaks, the `fury` cue.
  - `score.js`: composed per-stratum score with intensity layers 0/1/2 and a boss variant, fed by `game.js musicLevel`.
- **UI**: the "The Deep has changed" notice lists the v4 changes (shown to profiles of era < 3 that have runs).

## Remaining tasks (priority, reason)
1. **Hearth rule** (user decision 2026-10-23): max − smart = +10.0 against 8.95 allowed at n=240. Halving six stat terms and cutting rank caps did not move max. Untested suspects:
   - Deep Wick (dark loot through `--dark=smart`);
   - mastery 10;
   - the interaction with the hero EHP buffs (the gap was +1.7 before them).
   Rank-cap cuts need ember refunds first: the save decoder clamps ranks, so old codes would lose embers.
2. **Armory content** (user playtest note: the Relics page replaced by class/weapon meta upgrades): the page exists with empty lists; the design question is for the user.
3. **Path payoff**: an on-path boon (ΔlnD ≈ .03) loses to generic HP (.05–.1 at λ 2), so the knowledge gap rests on boss TTK rather than path synergies. The design question is for the user.
4. **Melee at bosses**: Knight 178 HP / 32 armor, Cleric 175 / 14 and Shade 165 HP with 35% dodge are stopgaps. Readable boss melee windows would let that EHP come back down.
- **Sheet model gaps found by the skill-fork audit** (the parity bands are only as true as the model; a scratch harness compared per-part sim damage on a lone dummy and a tight pack of 4):
  - Bone Knights cleave (strikes every foe within 30 of the target) is unmodelled. Modelling it puts r1/r2 above band, so it needs a minion retune with the model fix.
  - Hunter's Mark homing waves land about +18% of a Volley cast more than modelled.
  - The Shield Throw `return` part counts every pack foe; the sim reaches 3–5 of them.
  - Death Blossom: the model adds blossom cuts on n − 1 foes after a slash on all n; the sim cuts each foe once (shared `seen` set).
  - Burn may be counted twice (as a part total in `directDamage` and through `pool.fire` in `statusDamage`), and the Nova Burn counts once in the pack column.
  - The Volley pack rule counts min(n, 2 × arrows) hits; the sim lands about ×3 that on a tight pack (every Ranger fork).
  - The Hammer's base Judged +20% is missing from condFactor, while Judging Light assumes it.
  - Not verified against the sim: Twin Hammers second flight, Verdict burst, Veil of Night clone, Eclipse.
- **Route-fork follow-ups (6a)**:
  - Contagion (`r_el_b`) is pack-only. Outbreak reached a 120% share and Plaguebearer 85% to meet the parity mean, while the sheet ignores SPREAD_CAP (25% of the recipient's HP per pool). Give it a one-foe term, then cut Outbreak to ≤ 100%.
  - Rupture (`s_el_b`) carries `s1Dmg 0.3` ("Shadowstep deals +30%"), added because the sheet caps its share at 100%. Decide: keep it, or model the burst and remove it.
  - White Heat (`p_el_a`, IV r2 32.5, margin 1.0) bought its value with Burn +53% because the sheet ignores `searSkill` and `burnCrit`. Retune it once both are modelled.
  - Siege bolt: the model counts it hitting the whole pack; the measured reach is about 2. Necro `hb()` in `sheet.js` necroForms still uses the all-in-line pierce rule. PIERCE_LINE 0.5 was measured on 7869 Ballista shots.
- **Small code debts**: deeds are a `1 << i` 32-bit mask (`save.js`), which breaks at deed 32 while the save code allows 63 (26 used). The Aegis Charm's 0.4 s untouchable time after a block is undocumented.
- **In-class weapon spreads** (`sheet.js --weapons`, ±15% of the class median) were out before v4 and are unchanged: Knight warhammer ×0.70, Shade daggers ×0.64 / knives ×2.14, Cleric lantern ×2.95, Necro bone wand ×3.37, Pyro ember wand ×1.83. Each needs a per-class weapon dmg pass with a TTK re-read.
- **TTK sizing**: use `--from=4` reads, not the sheet formula. Necro TTK elasticity is high (weapon ×1.5 → TTK ×0.69, through raises); Knight and Cleric weapon changes gave 0.4–0.55 of their dps change.
- **Shade room damage**: armor 5 → 7 barely moved HP lost per combat room (8–15% per fork, against 1–8% for the other heroes). The landing grace fixed its boss deaths, not its room intake.
