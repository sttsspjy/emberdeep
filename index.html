Settings

Click a key, then press the new key or mouse button. Esc cancels, Backspace clears.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick aims (let go and you aim where you walk), X / RT attacks, RB / B skill 1, LT / Y skill 2, A / LB dashes. Use RB and LT for aimed skills: your thumb stays on the stick. Ground skills land farther the harder you push the right stick. Back opens the character sheet (LB / RB switch tabs, B closes it); Start pauses. In a boon offer, X takes the card and Y rerolls.
